# NetNet Pendle DETF Operations Matrix

Best-effort council consolidation originally against PRD v0.12, reconciled with subsequent human deployment/first-bond decisions and **PRD v0.15 atomic rollover, Weighted LP modes and USDG bond locks**, 2026-09-25. Proposed opening/expansion numbers remain distinct from unresolved expansion mechanics. Research specification, not implementation/deployment authorization; explicit human decisions control.

**Reading rules**
- `UNKNOWN` means that cell cannot yet be filled reliably. It is not permission to select a default. Populated cells state known behavior at the product level; they do not imply verified numerical parameters, final selectors or deployable calculations.
- **Public** = user-facing operation; **internal** = execution step of another operation, not a new public endpoint; **conditional** = variant identified for completeness but not fully selected/specified. Multiple rows may share implementation or compose atomically.
- **DETF** = NET-DETF; **sDETF** = sNET-DETF staking receipt, not external sNET; **HLP** = custom hook LP; **PLP** = Pendle market LP; **VLP** = canonical NET/USDG V2 LP; **SE shares** = custom NetNet V2 SE shares, not VLP itself.
- **SWAP**, **BURN**, **BOND**, **HLP-EXIT** and **NET-ENTRY/USDG-ENTRY** refer to the defined policies below. `P` denotes NET per DETF; `p` denotes the WAD-scaled contraction incentive. They are not the same variable.
- Unspecified fee cells are `UNKNOWN`, never an assumed zero. Known oracle lookup policy is recorded without inventing fee percentages. There is no new strict-conformance certification prerequisite.

## Defined policies and known calculation references

These definitions record selected behavior; they do not fill unresolved numerical parameters by assumption.

1. **NET-ENTRY:** NET/sNET payments for selected swaps/bonds acquire Keep-YT exposure: supported SY entry, PT/SY market liquidity and retained YT. Exact split/settlement math must follow the selected strategy specification. **USDG-ENTRY:** acquire configured custom NetNet V2 SE shares representing canonical NET/USDG VLP; hook holds those SE shares. One payment cannot fund both destinations.
2. **SWAP:** transfer existing tokens, with no exchange-attributable DETF mint/burn. Ordinary NET/sNET output uses interest only, including once-counted claimed cash; USDG uses its configured SE leg. Use selected Weighted math. Exact custom balance/rate mapping and fee order remain unresolved; public-swap rows do not silently inherit the owned-reserve burn domain.
3. **Standard withdrawal branch:** P >= 1 NET/DETF uses SWAP, including equality; P < 1 uses eligible BURN. The authoritative price measure remains UNKNOWN. This predicate is not imposed on raw public hook swaps. ERC-4626 output is sNET only; SY/SE outputs are NET/sNET/USDG. No intermediate PT/YT/LP is an additional user output on those routes.
4. **BURN:** WAD=1e18; resolve `p = seigniorageIncentivePercentageOfVault(netDetfInstance)`; `qQuote = floor(q * (WAD + p) / WAD)`; quote `F(ownedReserveSnapshot, qQuote, tokenOut)`. Build the quote domain from DETF's actual owned HLP portion, not full-pool quoting followed by a cap. Receive and burn actual q only. Never mint, deposit or burn the virtual bonus. Actual realization cannot spend external LP allocations or exclusive user/fee assets. Insufficient delivery reverts all, without swap fallback, partial payout or pending entitlement. Exact owned-book construction, funding order and exact-output inverse remain UNKNOWN.
5. **Dedicated reinvestment:** same ownership-limited burn calculation, but always `qQuote=q`, in all price regimes; then normal BOND contribution. This excludes the contraction input bonus, not normal bond fees/splits. Independently permitted contraction and a later bond keep their normal economics. This is not proof that all possible transaction cycles are unprofitable.
6. **BOND:** use the selected Universal V4 calculation chain unchanged unless concretely incompatible: `UniswapV4DetfCommon.sol` purchase/matching/split helpers, `DETFBondNFTMathLib.sol` duration terms, and `DETFFundedBondTarget.sol` funded-position processing. The latter receives already calculated principal. Reference linear principal vesting does not override the custom maturity cliff. Oracle minimum-duration compatibility with a next-epoch release remains unresolved; no silent extension or fabricated bonus duration.

   **FIRST-BOND — explicit reference calculation (PRD §10.4):** `A` is the lead payment in the selected reserve leg's native units after reference conversion. `P0` is WAD-scaled reserve-leg tokens per DETF, using `openingOfPair` or `creationOfPair` if opening is zero. `M` is the existing oracle-derived WAD duration multiplier; `p` is the WAD seigniorage fraction. With `WAD=1e18`:

   ```text
   Q(x) = floor(nativeToWad(pair, x) * 1e9 / P0)
   G = Q(A)
   U = Q(floor(A * M / WAD))
   B = floor(U * (WAD - p) / WAD)
   R = floor(U * p / WAD) + floor(G * p / WAD)
   actual DETF minted across these components = G + B + R
   ```

   `G` is the unboosted reserve self-leg, `B` the buyer's staked bond principal, and `R` the funded staking-reward allocation. `U` is only the gross purchase quote used for splitting, not an extra mint. Preserve each floor and native-unit conversion; the bond duration multiplier is applied once, not stacked with an ordinary-mint `1+p` uplift. Reward distribution, not the opening quote, determines the first buyer's share of R.

   The reference Weighted hook requires a full-book first join. Additional required non-DETF legs are pulled within the same bond transaction, sized by `wadToNative(otherPair, floor(G * P0_other / 1e9))`; the lead payment is not necessarily the whole bootstrap contribution. Join nonzero initial LP before activation; later bond/reward-funding failure rolls everything back. Preserve custom direct HLP custody, cliff release and NET-synchronized epoch behavior.

Illustration only of the formula, not current launch parameters: lead payment 1,000, opening price 1, multiplier 1.10, p=10% gives G=1,000, U=1,100, B=990 and R=210 DETF. Additional full-book payments are not included. The owner rejects the former 1 NET opening proposal and now proposes **1,000 NET per DETF at launch plus 0.5% expansion per NET epoch**. Do not change the ongoing 1 NET peg by implication; supply basis, eligibility/stop rule and catch-up compounding remain UNKNOWN.
7. **HLP-EXIT:** proportional mode allocates `floor(h*X/H)` per included component. Unbalanced/subset and single-token modes over NET/sNET/USDG/DETF are also selected and use the copied Weighted invariant/share-debit calculation, fees and actual delivery; do not force them through a proportional-only output cap.
