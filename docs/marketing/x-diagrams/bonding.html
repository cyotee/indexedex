Drill-down · Bonding

Lock liquidity. Get an NFT. Claim while it matures.

Mint and burn alone cannot build the book. Bonding is a timed deposit: the buyer’s payment plus newly minted $DTF-DETF join the reserve together. The buyer gets a bond NFT. A cut of all minted DETF goes to bond holders, more for a longer lock, claimable while the bond is still locked.

IndexedEx

Why bond

Mint and burn are not enough

The DETF needs committed liquidity

Primary mint and burn move size in and out. The reserve still needs deposits that stay for a set time, so the book has depth that is not leaving on the next trade.

Most efficient way to add LP

Payment plus free $DTF-DETF

When someone buys a bond, a proportional amount of $DTF-DETF is minted to join their payment as liquidity. The buyer does not have to buy that DETF on the market. They receive it into the LP while the bond is locked.

$DTF in → mint matching $DTF-DETF → both join reserve LP

Who gets a bond NFT

Bond buyer

NFT for a timed lock

The buyer receives a Bond NFT. It records the lock, their share of reserve LP, and the weight that earns rewards. Principal stays locked until maturity.

DETF creator

NFT that never unlocks

Creating a DETF also mints a creator bond. It is permanently locked. The creator cannot cash the principal. They can claim a portion of the DETF minted to bond holders, same claim path as any other bond.

Rewards while the bond is locked

Incentive

A cut of all minted DETF

A portion of every $DTF-DETF mint goes to bond holders. That is the reward for locking liquidity, not a cash-out of the bond.

Lock length

Longer lock, larger share

The longer the buyer commits, the greater their portion of those rewards relative to other bonds.

While it matures

Claim on the NFT

Those extra rewards are claimable while the bond is still locked. Claiming is not closing. Amounts are not guaranteed.

When the bond matures, the buyer may close

1

Withdraw the LP

The DETF pulls that bond’s liquidity out of the reserve pool.

2

Return their share

The buyer gets back the liquidity for their share of the reserve: the $DTF side, through the SE vault. Not a full dump on the $DTF / $DTF-DETF book.

3

Protocol keeps the DETF

The $DTF-DETF from that LP is not returned to the buyer. It is redeposited so the protocol owns it. Bonding is how the DETF acquires lasting DETF liquidity.
