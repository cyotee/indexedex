Drill-down · Mint, burn, expansion

Mint and burn on the reserve curve. Bonds get a cut.

Protocol mint uses the same constant-product curve as the $DTF / $DTF-DETF pool, with an incentive on the payment so minting here beats buying on the market. The $DTF paid is deposited in the SE vault and added to liquidity. Burn is the inverse. Whenever $DTF-DETF is minted, a percentage is minted to bond holders for locking that liquidity.

IndexedEx

Mint

User pays

$DTF in

The payment is deposited in the SE vault and added as double-sided liquidity in $DTF / ETH. That is the same vault the reserve already uses as its $DTF book.

Same curve as the reserve

Mint $DTF-DETF + incentive

Price follows the reserve pool’s constant-product curve. An incentive is applied to the payment so the user gets more $DTF-DETF than by buying it on Uniswap.

$DTF → SE vault LP → mint $DTF-DETF at CP + incentive

Why mint here

Better than buying the pool

The market can always buy $DTF-DETF on $DTF / $DTF-DETF. The protocol mint is built to win that flow: same curve, extra DETF on the payment, and the $DTF stays in the vault as LP.

Burn

User pays

$DTF-DETF in

The protocol burns that DETF. This is the inverse of mint, not a sale on the Uniswap book.

Same curve, inverse

Pay $DTF out + incentive

Buy-back follows the same reserve constant-product curve. An incentive is applied so the user gets more $DTF than by selling $DTF-DETF on the market. $DTF comes out through the SE vault (zap out).

burn $DTF-DETF → SE vault zap out → $DTF at CP + incentive

Why burn here

Better than selling the pool

Selling on $DTF / $DTF-DETF is always available. Protocol burn is built to win that flow: same curve, extra $DTF on the way out, liquidity pulled from the vault instead of a full market dump.

Bond holders and automatic expansion

On every mint

A percentage to bonds

Whenever $DTF-DETF is minted, a cut is minted to bond holders. That is the reward for locking liquidity in the reserve. It is not cashing the bond principal.

Automatic expansion

Extra DETF to the same bonds

The protocol can also mint new $DTF-DETF to bond holders without a user mint. Same destination: the bond NFT reward ledger, for liquidity that stays locked.

Who can take it

Claim on the bond NFT

That DETF accrues to the NFT. The owner can claim it while the bond is still locked. Amounts are not guaranteed.
