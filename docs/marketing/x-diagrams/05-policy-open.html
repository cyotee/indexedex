Mint and burn rules

Policy or Open

Set at deploy. This is about when you may mint or burn against the reserve, not a promise the token stays at a price.

IndexedEx

Default

Policy

  • Mint is allowed when the contract price is above the mint line.
  • Burn is allowed when it is below the burn line.
  • Inside the band, use the market, not primary mint or burn.

Deploy option

Open

  • No price restrictions on mint or burn.
  • You can mint and burn regardless of contract price.
  • Fees can still apply. Open is not a peg.

Contract price is the DETF token versus a pair in the reserve, not a dollar price.
