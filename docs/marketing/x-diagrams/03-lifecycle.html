Lifecycle

Inert until the first bond

A new DETF does nothing until someone bonds. That first bond turns it live and puts assets into protocol-owned reserve.

IndexedEx

1

Deploy

The DETF exists. It is inert. Mint and burn are blocked. No owner can patch it later.

2

First bond

Someone locks accepted tokens and receives a bond NFT. The DETF goes live. Protocol-owned reserve starts.

3

Use it

Hold the token. Mint and burn against the reserve. Bond more. Claim rewards on a bond you own.

After deploy, a DETF instance stays unowned. Bad config means ship a new one.
