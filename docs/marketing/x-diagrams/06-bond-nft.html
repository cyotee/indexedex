Bond NFT

Rewards now, principal later

A bond is an NFT you own. DETF can accrue to it while it is locked. Claim that DETF any time. Principal waits for maturity.

IndexedEx

The position

Your bond NFT

Records the lock and the weight that earns rewards. The protocol also holds its own bond. That one is not yours to claim.

→

While locked

Claim rewards

Owner-only. Sends accrued DETF to you. Does not end the bond. Pending can be zero and a claim still runs.

→

After maturity

Sell or close

Then you can sell the bond into the protocol or close it. That is the principal path. Not the claim button.

Creating a DETF also issues a creator bond. That bond cannot be cashed out.
