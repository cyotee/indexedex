Why the SE vault cost matters here

Sell on CP. Buy on a cheaper line.

The reserve sells $DTF as if it were a normal constant-product pool. It gets that $DTF from the SE vault: withdraw first, then trade only the leftover, or pay from inventory at no extra swap. That acquire path stays under the constant-product curve. The gap is one place DETFs take a profit. Amounts are not guaranteed.

Fees in the SE vault pull the reserve price

1

Vault earns swap fees

Double-sided LP in $DTF / ETH collects market-maker fees. The SE vault token is worth more $DTF.

2

Rate provider updates

Each vault token now prices as more $DTF. The reserve book has more $DTF on the vault side without a matching DETF mint.

3

$DTF looks cheaper

That extra $DTF claim lowers the vault token’s price in the reserve pool versus $DTF-DETF.

4

Market buys through the vault

Traders take the cheaper $DTF from this pool. Flow goes through the SE vault, not a full buy on $DTF / ETH.
