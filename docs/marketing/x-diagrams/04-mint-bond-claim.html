Three paths

Mint, bond, and claim

Mint pays a token the DETF accepts and receives DETF. Bond locks the same kind of token for a bond NFT. Claim takes DETF that accrued to that bond.

IndexedEx

Pay in, token out

Mint

You pay an accepted token. You receive DETF. Burn is the reverse, when the DETF is live and the mode allows it.

Lock in, NFT out

Bond

You lock an accepted token for a set number of days. You get a bond NFT. You cannot cash the principal until it matures.

Rewards, not exit

Claim

The owner can take DETF that accrued to the bond while it is still locked. Claiming is not cashing the bond out.

Sell or close principal only after the lock ends. Amounts are not guaranteed.
