Drill-down · Rebasing claim token

Share in bond rewards without a timed lock

The rebasing token, or claim token, is how anyone can take part in bond rewards by giving the DETF its own token to buy into liquidity. It owns a Bond NFT, so it earns a portion of all minted $DTF-DETF, like other bond holders, but a lesser amount than timed bonds.

The rebasing token owns the protocol Bond NFT. Same reward ledger as other bonds. A portion of all minted $DTF-DETF accrues here, and holders of the claim token share in it.

Vs timed bonds

Lesser share than lockers

Timed bond buyers commit for days and get a larger portion, more for a longer lock. The claim token’s bond has no timed commitment from you, so it earns a lesser amount than those holders.
