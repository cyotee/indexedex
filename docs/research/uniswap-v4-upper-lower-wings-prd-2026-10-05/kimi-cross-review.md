## Corrections and adjudications

1. **Token orientation — MiniMax is inverted; historical evidence located.** MiniMax §0 defines upper wing = token1-only, lower = token0-only. V4 mechanics are the opposite: a range entirely **above** current tick holds **token0** (`getLiquidityForAmount0` applies when `sqrtPrice ≤ sqrtPriceA`); a range **below** holds **token1**. Direct historical evidence (task asked to locate it): `contracts/protocols/dexes/uniswap/v4/UNISWAP_V4_STANDARD_EXCHANGE_FULL_RANGE_DEPLOYED_BOOK_PRD.md:70-81` — center ≈ ±1 tickSpacing (`centerWidthMultiplier = 2`); "**90%** of deployable **token1** in the **lower** wing, **90%** of deployable **token0** in the **upper** wing" (`activeLiquidityBps = 1000`); steady state ≈ 20% sleeve / 72% wings / 8% center; ticks frozen at first create. Any PRD diagram must use: **upper wing = token0-only, lower wing = token1-only.**
2. **Full-range center + wings is a NEW hybrid, not a restoration.** MiniMax (§2.1/2.2) makes the center full-range and adds wings. The historical three-position book was a **finite** center (±1 spacing) + wing envelope. Grok (OD-1) and Astra (§2) correctly refuse to choose. Since the owner requires "restored three-position intent," the restoration candidate is the finite-center historical book; the full-range-center hybrid must be labeled new design. Both are owner-visible options, not defaults.
3. **MiniMax OD-1 recommendation (disallow single-token entries in v1) conflicts with the owner's explicit requirement** of "useful single-token composability, not banning scalar entry." Reject that recommendation. Scalar entry stays in scope; its admission rule and manipulation controls are proposed-below, not consensus-safe.
4. **Sleeve formula — Astra's correction verified against primary source.** Zap PRD D3 (`docs/plans/UNISWAP_V4_STANDARD_EXCHANGE_PROPORTIONAL_ZAP_IN_PRD.md:32`): sleeve target = oracle % of **owned deployed principal**, and `:143` `targetFree = floor(T·p/(1e18+p))` — i.e. `F = p·D`, **not** `p·(F+D)` as LOCAL_BUFFER D17 states. The wings PRD must cite the deployed-principal base (current source law) and note the documentary conflict; for three positions, `D = Σ` over center+wings.
5. **Weighted matrix dimensions — Astra's correction adopted.** Weighted accepts `2 ≤ n ≤ 8` total legs (`weighted/…DFPkg.sol:373-400`): cover buffered `b = 1..8` with raw `r = 0..8−b` subject to `2 ≤ b+r ≤ 8`; 8 buffered + any raw is invalid; a mandatory raw DETF self-leg caps buffered at 7 for n=8 DETF bindings. This refines my original's "1..8 buffered × raw combinations" and Grok's minimal-rows caution into a concrete bounded grid.
6. **Protection constants (25/50/10/1 bp) are FullSpread's** (`H/TransitionPlanner:19-24` per Astra). Copying them is a *proposal*, not inheritance; own-book composition swaps crossing the vault's own wing liquidity are unanalyzed under them. Adopt as proposed starting values pending owner confirmation — MiniMax's silent "adopted as law" overstates.
7. **Gas: per-transaction, chain-specific.** Astra adds EIP-7825 (16,777,216 tx gas where active) — a blanket 31M/32M assumption is wrong without per-chain verification. Grok OD-10 (name chain, measure top-level tx cold/warm for first mint, join, exit, blocked sleeve exit, rebalance) is the right acceptance shape. No numeric pass line exists today.
8. **No arbitrary SUT mocks — clarified, not violated.** MiniMax's `test/harness/…ConsumingHook.sol` is a *consumer harness*, permissible under LOCAL_BUFFER D19 (generic unlock harness + one real hook); the ban is on mocking the SUT (vault/pool/oracle). Keep the distinction explicit so a later agent doesn't "fix" the harness as a mock violation.
9. **Astra surface additions adopted:** `IStandardExchangeTransitionQuote.sol:4-38,41-58` and `IStandardExchangeUnlockContextQuote.sol:8-16` (unavailable-unlock context) as required consumer-compatibility surfaces; blocked-state scalar pricing via local CP settlement (`StandardExchangeConstantProduct.sol:58-110`) is a *second economic mechanism* whose use while blocked needs explicit owner selection — neither sleeve nor TWAP field alone establishes safety.
