## Proposed ordinary-domain reachability argument for zero burn (INFERENCE)

Genesis establishes L=T and assigned-share sum=T-1000. Compound increases L without minting shares. Deposit mints floor(newL*T/L), at most newL when L>=T, preserving L>=T. A valid withdrawal s<=T-1000 removes floor(L*s/T); remaining gap is (L-T)-floor((L-T)*s/T)>=0. Transfers do not change L/T. Checked-arithmetic failures revert. Thus in supported successful flows, L>=T and any positive owned s burns at leasts>=1 liquidity. A zero-burn positive-share branch should not be manufactured by storage writes to claim coverage; document the domain proof and any actual alternative reachability if found. Oversized preview inputs bypass balance checks and remain separately testable; do not generalize this argument into NAV/asset safety.

Fee-zero, spacing1, initially tick0, with large paired input around2^100 raw units, permits total liquidity far above the1000 reserve while staying below ordinary int128 and per-tick caps (the actual calculated L/caps must be checked). Full assigned-share withdrawal leaves roughly1000 liquidity but large zap input. A residual manager swap toward the source sqrt-price limit can therefore stop before consuming that input, returning both currencies. Do not assume exact genesis L equals the input at this scale: compute the nested full-range rounding independently. Do not use a one-step constant-product or continuous-liquidity quote as an exact output oracle; tick-word traversal rounds by steps. A legitimate non-returning observer hook plus independent sizing/accounting checks or matched real-manager control fixture can supply signed swap observations. Future tests must prove limit reached and unwanted residual>0; no guaranteed full fill or guessed golden.
