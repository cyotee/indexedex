## 2. Audited findings across the three originals

**Orientation.** MiniMax's per-wing composition table (`minimax-original.md:35–44`) assigns token0 to below-range wings — inverted, same error as mine; its deposit flow targets `chosenLower = floor(tick/spacing)*spacing`, `chosenUpper = +spacing` (:62–63), which is the **current** tick bucket — in-range, not an out-of-range wing, contradicting its own direction definitions (:29).

**Share denominator / units — the decisive test of "do wings solve valuation."** MiniMax's Rank 1 mints `m = floor(S·amountIn/(B0+amountIn))` (:72). Algebra: post-deposit incumbents hold fraction `B0/(B0+c)` of the whole book, so their token1 becomes `(B0/(B0+c))·B1 < B1` — the depositor receives a claim on the token1 leg **without paying token1**. That is direct incumbent dilution whenever `B1 > 0`, and it is price-independent (token0 raw units vs `B0` only), so it cannot be a valuation rule. Additionally its own stated protection (:125, the 1 bp test with `B_i` pre-deposit) fails every single-token deposit since `C1 = 0` and `B1 > 0` — internally inconsistent. This is the concrete demonstration of Astra's :7 and Grok's §2: wing placement changes custody, not ownership; `min(floor(S·C0/B0), floor(S·C1/B1)) = 0` for any one-sided `C`, and wing liquidity units across unequal ranges are not interchangeable value (Grok :23). My original and Astra's and Grok's all agree on the correct conclusion; MiniMax's Rank 1 formula is the counterexample that proves why the question insisted on it.

**LP spot manipulation under oracle valuation.** Astra (:92) identifies the residual hole in its own Rank 1: even with an independent reference `p`, incumbent `D(q)` moves with pool spot, so `V` is manipulable through the pool; narrow wings sharpen it; the proposed upper/lower valuation envelopes are underived. I agree and strengthen: this is demonstrable (position value is a nonlinear function of `q`), so oracle admission + any pool-priced exit leg is extractable in one transaction unless charges/gates are calibrated across the whole permitted spot interval — an open quantification gate, not a disproof of all oracle designs (Astra's framing, which I adopt).

**Internal-market economics.** Convergence: internal CP admission is the only oracle-free synchronous scalar rule (me and Grok as Rank 1; Astra rank 3 on economic caution; MiniMax rank 3 fallback). Matched internal entry *and* exit keeps the book-price/pool-price hedge outside the SE (Grok :133); the remaining cost — divergence arbitrage against holders between consolidations, donation moving `B1/B0` — is agreed by all four and **unquantified by all four**. Spread charge λ and a divergence trigger remain uncalibrated owner policy. No fixed-state invariant is claimed as cross-state safety by any of us.

**Exact idle exit funding without search.** Convergent recipe: spend `F_out`; remove from the output-token wing only while strictly one-sided, sized by the closed-form liquidity-for-amount inverse; optional sized core removal (my step 3, disputed by Grok's simpler revert); final shortfall reverts atomically. Ceil/floor discipline: size with round-up, verify against round-down settlement (Grok :74). Astra's idle exit instead removes a proportional slice from *every* position and forward-swaps the known other entitlement once (:46–50) — legal (already-sized swap) but reintroduces a pool-priced exit leg beside oracle/CP-priced entry; under internal admission that pairing is exactly the hedge Grok excludes.
