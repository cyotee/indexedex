## 1. Disputed items — verified verdicts

1. **BasicVaultRepo = raw local balances, not economic eligibility — unanimous and confirmed.** `contracts/vaults/basic/BasicVaultRepo.sol:25–26,91–97`: `reserveOfToken` is for "locally held token reserves… NOT the owned shares of deployed liquidity reserves"; `_updateReserve` (:98–110) assigns an absolute value — no accrual, eligibility or provenance. Held-SY eligibility (interest vs contributed vs incentive vs principal-exit) and net-claimable amounts need separate accounting (C12). Agreed by all four.
2. **Twin Repo, same slot — confirmed (my finding, Astra concurs).** `MultiAssetBasicVaultRepo.sol:21` declares the identical `STORAGE_SLOT` (`keccak256("indexedex.vaults.basic")`) with equivalent layout; they are not independent books. Existing integrations use the twin with post-operation balance sync (`FeeCollectorSingleTokenPushTarget.sol:33`, `RocketPoolRETHStandardExchangeCommon.sol:339`, etc.).
3. **PLP/YT are locally held ERC20 balances — correction to MiniMax's reasoning.** MiniMax claims PLP/YT are "external positions… not 'locally held' by the hook" and thus outside the Repo's scope. The Repo's own example (BasicVaultRepo.sol:25–26) says the intended use IS "the locally held LP token balance"; PLP and YT are ERC20s held directly by the hook. What is out of scope is the *deployed-liquidity claim* those tokens represent and the sub-reserve's share accounting. Practical conclusions converge (owner mandates SY tracking; sub-reserve economics stay in separate accounting), but the custody characterization is corrected: raw PLP/YT balances *may* be snapshot for integrity checks; their underlying redemption value may *not* be booked there.
4. **Force-claim pretransfer theft — confirmed as a real gate (Astra's finding, verified by me).** `BasicVaultCommon.sol:80–104`: pretransfer credit requires `claimed <= U` where `U = balanceOf − reserveOfToken` (unbooked surplus); :46–54 absorbs unclaimed surplus into the book at route end. Pendle permits third-party claims paying the hook (`PendleYieldToken.sol:166–193`, previously verified; Astra cites :166–193 and `InterestManagerYT.sol:43–57,63–79` for fee-netted accrual). An unsolicited force-claim therefore appears as unbooked SY that a later pretransfer caller could be credited against, or that gets absorbed into reserves unattributed. Mitigations exist in-pattern (:75 requires `LocalCreditLib.requirePretransferCaller`), but the hook must reconcile known protocol receipts before contribution credit and sync every affected held-token boundary under one callback-safe transition (Astra §2; I adopt).
5. **Wrapper approximation vs actual Balancer BasePoolMath — Astra's mismatch is confirmed at the decisive point.** `UniswapV4StandardExchangeWeightedBufferHookMath.sol:482–487` self-describes its single-exact-output exit fee handling as "**approximate**: treat full amount as taxable" and grosses up `ceil(amountOut/(1−fee))`; :492–497 derives `invariantRatio ≈ (new/current)^w`. Balancer V3 computes the taxable nonproportional portion from the invariant with directed rounding (Context7 `/llmstxt/balancer_fi_llms-full_txt`, 2026-09-25: unbalanced = invariant-ratio BPT; single-token exact-out via `computeBalance`). I did not re-verify Astra's vendored `BasePoolMath.sol:126–205,277–339` line cites; the confirmed wrapper comment alone proves exact parity cannot be claimed from names. **Consequence:** C10's answer ("existing Balancer V3 Weighted unbalanced liquidity logic") must be implemented to BasePoolMath-faithful semantics with the wrapper's deviations (approximate exit fee, rounding) explicitly reconciled — compatibility work, not a C10 reopening. Grok's join-side "Mirror BasePoolMath" (`:303–353`) and MiniMax's mode inventory (`JoinCore.sol:343–810`; `ExitTarget.sol:8–67`; `IStandardExchangeMultiAssetLiquidity.sol:33–94`) stand for selector coverage, not numerical parity.
6. **NET pricing balance vs SY debit — Grok's sharper statement corrects my imprecision.** My original said a NET-out "changes both rated books." Correct semantics: the PLP/YT coordinate is untouched by SY-funded output, so the NET virtual (zap-out valuation) does not mechanically change; the sNET virtual (SY × rate) falls; the relative price moves without touching Pendle LP.
