## Disposition

requested-changes

The draft accurately preserves the adopted 49% sizing, first-deposit floor, executed exit receipt, seven standalone gas routes, nine composed gas files, principal preview-equality categories, and canonical dual proof. It does not yet state the complete union of required tests. Several retained family assertions are absent or still required only by reference. Migration row R02 also imposes a two-variable naming requirement that the remediation PRD expressly did not adopt.

My original proposal is therefore represented accurately on the central remediation rows, but incompletely on retained family coverage, hook adaptations, and consumer migration. The corrections below concern already adopted obligations, not new product choices.
