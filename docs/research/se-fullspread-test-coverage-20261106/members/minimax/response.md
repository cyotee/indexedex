## Assumptions and conventions

1. **Union rule.** Complete coverage is the union of the obligations already in the four documents. A family-plan or family-PRD obligation is never dropped because the published remediation plan is silent about it, and a remediation-plan obligation is never dropped because a family document is silent about it.
2. **"Keep or add" convention.** `keep` means the published remediation plan already carries this obligation (or an admitted document already names this exact row), and the coordinator's coverage section must retain it. `add` means the obligation is required by the family plan or family PRD and is absent from the published remediation plan, so the coverage section must add it. No row is marked as passing.
3. **Path prefixes.** `SPEC/` is `test/foundry/spec/vaults/standard/exchange/protocols/uniswap/v4/fullSpread/`. `HOOK/` is `test/foundry/spec/hooks/uniswap/v4/standardExchange/`. The seven spec files are named `UniswapV4FullSpreadStandardExchangeVault<Name>.t.sol` and are abbreviated below as `Package.t.sol`, `Money.t.sol`, `Native.t.sol`, `QuotesAndRate.t.sol`, `Callbacks.t.sol`, `AlreadyUnlocked.t.sol`, and `BufferHookIntegration.t.sol`.
4. **Assertion depth.** Every money, exit, and hook row must compare supply, holder balances, both positions, booked and reserved custody, wrapper balances, and per-address deltas. Asserting only that no tokens moved is not an assertion (family PRD FS-TEST-03, closing paragraph).
5. **Harness.** Production artifacts are built before the tests, because FactoryServices read creation bytecode from `out/`. The vault and the PoolManager are never mocked, etched, or storage-injected. Shares are bootstrapped only through the accepted public routes. `via_ir` is forbidden (family plan §3 and §13; family PRD FS-TEST-01).
6. **Naming of the nine hook row files.** The family plan section 12 names one row file per topology, plus the weighted three-leg file and the Balancer five-leg file. Those nine files are the composed-route set for gas, and they are the rows that carry the per-topology obligations.

## Coverage

### Gap 1 — every supported route, not a sampled route

The seven standalone economic routes are `exchangeIn` one-sided share mint, `exchangeIn` pass-through, `exchangeIn` one-token exit, `exchangeBundleInForSingleOut`, `exchangeBundleOut`, `donate`, and `compound`. The plan's owner answer withdraws the count of six and says a sampled route is not coverage. Each route is therefore given a success-shape row, a rejection row for its section 6 check order, and a gas row. `setOperator` and `setOperatorFor` are appointment calls and are not money routes, but their authorisation check is still a row (M-27).

### Gap 2 — preview equals execution

The family plan §10 makes the Planner the one engine for preview and execution, and the family PRD FS-OWN-30 requires the one-token exit quote to match the executed receipt and the both-token exit quote to match the executed outputs. The family plan §12 additionally requires `test_row_previewMatchesExecution` in every hook row, where the exact-in equality reads `amountOut` and the `previewExchangeOut` equality cannot pass as a positive quote. Preview-equals-execution rows are therefore named for the mint, the one-token exit, the both-token exit, the bundle-in, the pass-through, and each hook row. The zero `previewExchangeOut` is named as a negative preview row, never as a substitute for an equality.

### Gap 3 — integration with other vaults

The canonical cross-vault proof is the `dual/` row. A PairToken leg on another topology does not replace it, and `single/` is not a two-vault proof. Each of the nine row files is therefore named with its other leg (the PairToken identity record, a raw token, or this vault's own share in `single/`), which leg converts, and its assertion. The PairToken identity record is a single identity record used wherever a topology names another Standard Exchange; if the current package rejects the `token == se` bind without `asset()`, the row records the consumer, selector, arguments, and revert under FS-HOOK-04 and does not pass. `asset()` is never added to turn a rejected bind into a pass.

### Migration obligation

The preview-consumer migration remains a family-plan obligation. This ledger names it (P-20 through P-23) and names the checks that prove a migrated reader uses `amountOut`: the repo-wide compile gate, the six length gates the family PRD enumerates, the three ABI bindings it enumerates, the helper it enumerates, and the two production assignments the family plan names. The index at `docs/research/se-fullspread-preview-consumers-20261103/INDEX.md` has not been walked in this pass, and this ledger does not certify that migration.

## Limits

- This is a research product. It states the test obligations the four admitted documents already require. It does not implement any of them, does not approve implementation, does not replace the family plan, and does not authorise tests, builds, installs, deployment, or Git.
- No test was executed, no build was run, and no result is claimed as passing. "Keep" and "add" describe the coverage table, not a test outcome.
- No production or test source file was opened. Every path, row name, and function name is copied from an admitted document; the function names in M-41 and the file contents reported for `Money.t.sol` and `Package.t.sol` are the remediation PRD's report, not an independent re-read.
- The preview-consumer index was not walked. P-20 through P-23 name the obligation and the checks that prove a migrated reader uses `amountOut`; they do not certify the migration.
- The three "no" gaps in the brief are covered by rows M-15 through M-21 plus M-22 through M-27 (every supported route, not a sampled route), PE-01 through PE-11 (preview equals execution wherever the family plan or family PRD already requires it, including mint, bundle, pass-through, and the hook rows), and H-01 through H-21 (each row file, the other vault or raw leg, which leg converts, and the assertion, with `dual/` named as the canonical cross-vault proof).
- The closed product choices are preserved as written: the 49% processed-input swap with no iteration, no closed-form root, and no tick walk; the boundary and collapsed-candidate rules; the four-part exit receipt with `AmountOutNotMet` and no burn; the `exchangeRate` test withheld; the three gas ceilings; `seBooked()` reporting no balance; and the unresolved list left alone.
- The coordinator may add one explicit coverage section to `docs/research/se-fullspread-remediation-plan-20261106/IMPLEMENTATION_AND_TEST_PLAN.md`. That update must preserve the closed product choices, does not replace the family plan, and does not authorise implementation.
