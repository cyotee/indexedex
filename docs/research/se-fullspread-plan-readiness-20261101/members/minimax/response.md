## E4. Two locked rules re-authorize an audited mechanism, and the quote mechanism is unstated

**Usage fee (APEX-2026-044).** FS-OWN-24: "When shares are minted, if `feeTo` is not `address(0)`, mint `mulDiv(sharesMinted, usageFeeOfVault(address(this)), 1e18)` additional shares to `feeTo`." That is the finding's root cause verbatim. APEX-2026-044's root cause is `_mintWithUsageFee` minting the depositor's full `userShares` and then an extra `userShares * usageFee` to `feeTo`; HEAD still carries exactly that code in `contracts/vaults/standard/erc4626/ERC4626StandardExchangeCommon.sol:220-234`, annotated "Dilution usage fee on share-minting routes only (D40/D40a/D57). User receives full `userShares`; fee expands supply to feeTo when non-zero." The finding measured an LP loss of 9.38 against a griefer cost of 0.099 (about 94.79×) and an operator-set 100 % fee draining 92.96 % of LP value. Its fix requires three call-site changes the PRD never states: "Exact-in: check `minAmountOut` against net shares. Exact-out: gross up with `ceilDiv`. Previews: return net shares."

**Permissionless maintenance at live spot with an unbounded price limit (APEX-2026-023, APEX-2026-024).** FS-OWN-14 makes `compound()` permissionless and deploys booked idle; FS-MONEY-01 fixes the swap price limit at "`TickMath.MIN_SQRT_PRICE + 1` or `TickMath.MAX_SQRT_PRICE - 1`"; FS-OWN-15 says a redemption "may swap through the pool. The purpose is pool volume." APEX-2026-023's root cause is that maintenance sizes and mints the liquidity from the pool's live `slot0`, so an attacker who pushes the price inside their own unlock has the vault deploy at the displaced spot and books the impermanent loss onto honest LPs (executed PoCs: +25.05 and +68.09 for honest TVL 2000). Its smallest fix is "after the vault's own redemption swap, limit the trailing `_rebalanceLiquidReserveBestEffort()` to burn/refill only", and its defence in depth — "Bound the redemption swap with a price limit taken from the pre-operation price" and "Optionally, add a spot-vs-TWAP deviation guard before maintenance mints. This closes Route B but conflicts with D21 / l.351, so it needs owner authorization" — is exactly the authorization the new PRD does not carry. APEX-2026-024 is the same unbounded-limit shape on the redemption side (Wings family, but the mechanism is the limit, not the family).

**Quote mechanism.** The PRD names `UniswapV4FullSpreadStandardExchangeVaultProjection.sol`, `...Planner.sol`, `...Math.sol` and `...Execution.sol`, and FS-OWN-18 puts the V4 Quoter and State View in the aware repo, but it never says how the entry and redemption swaps are priced, whether a step cap exists, what happens when the cap is hit, or whether a partial fill is paid. APEX-2026-041's root cause is exactly "`UniswapV4Quoter.quoteFromState(maxSteps = 64)` and reverts `QuoteWorkLimit()` if a quote is not filled within 64 steps", with the PoC showing a zap deposit ceiling falling from 51.43e18 to 0.636e18 after 300 dust positions; its fix offers "return the partial fill instead of reverting" or a gas-calibrated step bound, and "Prefer tickSpacing ≥ 60, or enforce a minimum spacing". APEX-2026-026 is the same class on the Wings quote. The new PRD does not constrain tick spacing: FS-OWN-22 keeps "only that the pool is initialized on the bound manager and the hook is `address(0)`" and deletes "outside the inherited domain", and FS-PKG-02's `InvalidPoolKey` list has no spacing term. APEX-2026-051 is the analytic-planner branch of the same surface.

# Conclusions

**Verdict: not ready.** The locked requirements are strong enough to plan almost everything, and the plan should not wait on anything else. Three product decisions are still open in the locked PRD, and all three sit exactly where the second audit found exploitable behaviour, which means the plan cannot both implement the locked rules and discharge the owner's third scope item ("test that the second-audit vulnerabilities are not present in this new vault") without an owner answer. The plan author would otherwise have to choose, and that choice is product behaviour.

**B1 — Is the usage fee minted on top of the depositor's shares, or carved out of them?**

- PRD sentence: FS-OWN-24 — "When shares are minted, if `feeTo` is not `address(0)`, mint `mulDiv(sharesMinted, usageFeeOfVault(address(this)), 1e18)` additional shares to `feeTo`. That is the Vault Fee Oracle usage fee."
- Why it blocks: this is APEX-2026-044's root cause, and HEAD still carries that code in `ERC4626StandardExchangeCommon._mintWithUsageFee`. The finding measured LP loss at about 94.79× the griefer's cost and an operator-set 100 % fee taking 92.96 % of LP value. The PRD is also silent on the three call-site consequences the audit's fix names — whether `previewExchangeIn` returns net or gross shares and whether `minAmountOut` is checked against net or gross. A test that "the second-audit vulnerability is not present" has no expected value while FS-OWN-24 stands as written, and the PRD nowhere records that the owner accepts this exposure.
- Owner question: "Does FS-OWN-24 keep the usage fee minted on top of the depositor's shares — the APEX-2026-044 pattern, where existing holders absorb the fee — or must the plan implement the audited form, in which the depositor receives `grossShares - feeShares`, `feeTo` receives `feeShares`, and `previewExchangeIn` and the `minAmountOut` check both use the net amount?"

**B2 — Does the permissionless compound and the pool swap keep the unbounded price limit, and may they deploy at the live spot?**

- PRD sentences: FS-OWN-14 — "`compound()` deploys booked idle… Any address may call it."; FS-MONEY-01 — "The entry swap, when one is needed, uses `_swap`. Its price limit is `TickMath.MIN_SQRT_PRICE + 1` or `TickMath.MAX_SQRT_PRICE - 1`."; FS-OWN-15 — "A redemption takes deployed liquidity first and may swap through the pool. The purpose is pool volume."
- Why it blocks: this is APEX-2026-023's configuration (maintenance mints at a displaced spot after an attacker pushes the price inside their own unlock; the executed PoCs charged the impermanent loss to honest LPs) and APEX-2026-024's shape (a redemption selling the whole opposing balance at a MIN/MAX limit). The audit's defence in depth says a spot-versus-TWAP deviation guard "needs owner authorization"; the new PRD authorizes the opposite and records no acceptance. A regression test for 023 and 024 has no expected value while the rules stand.
- Owner question: "Does the locked rule stand — permissionless `compound()` deploys idle at the live pool price, and the entry and redemption swaps use `MIN_SQRT_PRICE + 1` / `MAX_SQRT_PRICE - 1` with no pre-operation price limit and no spot-versus-TWAP deviation guard — or must the plan add the APEX-2026-023 bound (a price limit derived from the pre-operation price, and a deployment cap) even though that departs from Weir's `_swap` limit?"

**B3 — How are the entry and redemption swaps priced, and is any tick spacing accepted?**

- PRD sentences: FS-OWN-22 — "The pool-key check is only that the pool is initialized on the bound manager and the hook is `address(0)`." with FS-PKG-02's `InvalidPoolKey` list carrying no spacing term, and §1.2/§3 which name `...Projection.sol`, `...Planner.sol`, `...Math.sol` and `...Execution.sol` and put the V4 Quoter and State View in the aware repo (FS-OWN-18) without naming a pricing method, a step cap, a fallback, or a partial-fill rule.
- Why it blocks: APEX-2026-041 and APEX-2026-026 are quote-surface DoS findings — a bounded step count with a revert and no fallback, which dust positions next to spot turn into a freeze of deposit and redeem — and APEX-2026-051 is the analytic branch of the same surface, where the impact gate is a no-op and a preview promises shares that execution rejects. The plan must write the quote mechanism, its bound, its fallback and the spacing rule; each of those is product behaviour (does a dust-seeded pool freeze this vault?), and the PRD is silent on all four.
- Owner question: "For the entry and redemption swap previews, does the vault price with the V4 Quoter's bounded `quoteFromState`, with the analytic planner, or with a bounded exact-in fallback — and when the bound is hit, does the route revert or pay a partial fill — and does `deployVault` accept any tick spacing or enforce a minimum, as APEX-2026-041 and APEX-2026-026 require?"

**What is already settled and must not be re-opened by the plan.** The dispatch paths and their settlement rules (FS-UNLOCK-01/02/03); the absence of `getRate`, `rateAsset` and the whole Standardized Yield surface (FS-OWN-03, FS-OWN-05); the sleeve target, the 5 % tolerance and the best-effort refill (FS-OWN-04, FS-OWN-12); the deployed-first redemption and its borrowed-path variant (FS-OWN-15, FS-OWN-30, FS-OWN-31); the `MIN_SHARES` floor at `address(0)` (FS-OWN-11, FS-MONEY-01); the kill switch (FS-OWN-26); the deadline and disabled errors (FS-OWN-28, FS-OWN-26); the `exchangeIn` return ABI and its selectors (FS-OWN-08); the native boundary (FS-OWN-17, read together with FS-A10's "the inherited wrapper boundary"); the aware-repo component set (FS-OWN-18) and the position repo (FS-OWN-02, FS-OWN-06).

**Items the plan must state but that need no owner answer.** These are documentation or scope statements the plan author can make from the sources, and leaving them implicit would be a defect in the plan rather than an open product choice: the zero-share-mint guard that APEX-2026-034's fix requires and FS-MONEY-01 does not state; the EIP-712 domain name and version for the `erc2612Facet` that FS-FACET-01 installs and the PRD does not specify; the emitted event set, since FS-OWN-09 keeps paired events on the mapped routes while FS-OWN-14 fixes only `Compounded(address indexed token, uint256 amount)` and the function-review file leaves the paired event names as "renamed equivalents"; the home of `UnderlyingUnavailable(address,uint256,uint256)`, which exists in no interface at HEAD, is called "a shared Standard Exchange error" by FS-OWN-31, and is absent from the family error list in §3 — the plan must say whether it lands in Crane's shared `IStandardExchangeErrors` (a submodule change) or in the family interface; the two matrix deviations forced by this family (the exact-out row tests, and "booked" versus FS-OWN-32's refund) and the decimal-row scope; the stale matrix-PRD pointer to a deleted "V4 adversarial TestBase"; and the stale `0x05562cc8` comment in Crane's `IStandardExchangeIn`, which must not be copied.

**Answer to the question as asked.** Not yet. Once B1, B2 and B3 are answered, the locked requirements are sufficient: the matrix extension, the integration rows and the audit-regression suite are all specifiable from the PRD, the matrix PRD and the finding texts, with no further product choice. I did not write the plan and I did not invent the owner's answers.
