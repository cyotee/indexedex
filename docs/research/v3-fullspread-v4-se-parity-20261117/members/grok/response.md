## Conclusions

Q1 verdict: plan not accurate.

Q2 verdict: not confirmed.

V4 vault used for the verdict: `contracts/vaults/standard/exchange/protocols/uniswap/v4/upperLowerWings/`. That is the supported Uniswap V4 SE family named at `CLAUDE.md` line 24, and its production contracts implement Standard Exchange deposit, redeem, exact-out, rate, and standardized-yield behavior. `v4/fullSpread` production source is also present and implements a different Standard Exchange vault. The router says that family is deleted and must not be restored. The files were read. They were not treated as absent, and they were not treated as the supported V4 SE vault. Pair-token is not the vault for this question.

The plan does not ask this council's question. At plan lines 3-5 it asks how to make the V3 vault satisfy the locked PRD. That PRD, lines 11-13 and 29-31, names the Uniswap V4 full-spread vault as the behavior source and says upper/lower wings are not the template. That difference is evidence. It is not adopted as a substitute question. Satisfying the PRD is not the same claim as defining V4 SE vault behavior.

Behaviors that match the identified wings vault: none of the economic results the question lists. The only overlaps are surface gates that do not carry the wings result. Both check a deadline before money work (`Money.sol` line 45; plan step 4). A wings deposit reverts when the registry reports the vault disabled (`Money.sol` line 125), and the plan also blocks a non-withdrawal execution route while disabled. Those are not the same receipt, the same error, or the same exception list. Wings redeem, as read, does not check disable (`Money.sol` lines 147-176).

Behaviors that do not match:

- Share issuance and floor. The plan requires raw-liquidity shares, a first add above 1,000, and `balanceOf(address(0))` of 1,000 inside `totalSupply`, with `grossShares = addedLiquidity - 1,000` (plan step 1 and step 2; PRD V3-ACC-02 and V3-ACC-03). Wings issues `credit` on an empty vault and `floor(supply * credit / value)` afterward, and states that there is no permanent sink (`Accounting.sol` lines 8-9 and 18-22). The deposit path mints that amount to the recipient (`Money.sol` lines 128-134). No 1,000 floor was observed.
- Fee taking. The plan takes the usage fee out of `grossShares`, caps it at `3e17`, and mints fee shares to `feeTo` (plan step 2; PRD V3-ACC-04). Wings `issue` has no fee argument, and the deposit path read mints the full issued amount to the recipient. The wings package inserts a USAGE fee-type id (`DFPkg.sol` lines 52-53). That declaration is not a share-fee deduction in the issuance path read.
- One-token deposit. The plan, on an existing supply and an interior price, swaps `floor(processedInput * 49 / 100)` once at the min or max sqrt limit and mints liquidity that consumes the output (plan step 2; PRD V3-ACC-05). Wings deposit accepts only the target token, prices shares from liquidation value, and then places inventory (`Money.sol` lines 24, 37-39, and 112-136; `QuoteBase.sol` lines 27-32).
- Both-token deposit. The plan adds the greatest proportional liquidity and does not swap, on `exchangeBundleInForSingleOut` (plan step 7; PRD V3-ACC-07). The examined wings interface and money surface have no bundle deposit. Wings money is target-only.
- Exits. The plan burns the caller's liquidity fraction and pays principal, fee, and booked claims, swapping the whole opposing claim or reverting without a burn (plan step 4; PRD V3-ACC-12 and V3-ACC-13). Wings redeem pays target-token value entitlement and can close core and both wings, then swap a local deficit (`Money.sol` lines 147-208).
- Pass-through. The plan's underlying-to-underlying `exchangeIn` mints no shares and takes no fee (plan step 4; PRD V3-ACC-14). Wings `exchangeIn` rejects every route that is not target-to-share or share-to-target (`QuoteBase.sol` lines 27-32).
- Reserves and booked versus unbooked. The plan accounts deployed liquidity, unpaid fees, booked remainders, and unbooked balances separately. `reserves()` is principal plus unpaid fees, not booked balances (plan steps 1 and 6; PRD V3-ACC-08 and V3-ACC-09). It removes the liquid sleeve. Wings keeps a liquid-reserve percentage sleeve (`Accounting.sol` lines 41-44; `Harvest.sol` lines 157-165), reports `localReserves`, and treats local target as the pretransfer booked amount (`Money.sol` lines 234-237). Its repo has no booked-remainder array (`Repo.sol` lines 14-24).
- Maintenance. The plan adds `donate` and `compound`, uses the 49 percent shape or a proportional add, and removes `rebalanceLiquidReserve` (plan step 7; PRD V3-OWN-02). Wings has `rebalance` and harvest placement. Its examined interface does not declare `donate` or `compound`.
- Import. The plan keeps NFT import and says V4 has no import (plan step 8; PRD V3-OWN-01). Wings, as examined, has no import. Keeping import is a labeled V3 product decision in the PRD, not a copy of wings, and it is extra behavior relative to the identified vault.
- Quotes. The plan keeps two-word `previewExchangeIn`, refuses `getRate`, and removes `quoteAssets` (plan steps 4 and 5; PRD V3-ACC-19 and V3-OWN-05). Wings `getRate` and `exchangeRate` are value per share (`EconomicTarget.sol` lines 27-29; `SYTarget.sol` lines 30-32). Wings `previewExchangeIn` hardcodes the refund word to 0 (`InTarget.sol` lines 13-16). Wings installs `quoteAssets` (`TransitionTarget.sol` line 18).
- Disable and pool-lock or unlock. The plan reverts `UniswapV3Exchange_BoundPoolInteractionBlocked` when `pool.slot0().unlocked` is false, and it forbids `unlockCallback` (plan steps 0 and 4; PRD V3-ACC-15). Wings mutates through `unlockCallback` (`ExecutionDelegate.sol` line 22). When the manager is already unlocked, wings redeem pays from local target instead of modifying positions (`Money.sol` lines 167-170). That is the opposite gate, and the plan does not keep the wings local-cash result.
- Exact-out. The plan's `previewExchangeOut` returns 0 and does not revert with a vault error. Enabled `exchangeOut` reverts `ExchangeOutNotAvailable` and moves no tokens (plan step 5; PRD V3-ACC-17). Wings `exchangeOut` performs the exact-out deposit or redeem, and its preview returns the required input (`OutTarget.sol` lines 8-24).

V4-only mechanism, named rather than required as a copy: wings position changes and deficit swaps depend on PoolManager unlock. The plan forbids `unlockCallback` and expresses pool actions as V3 `swap`, `mint`, `burn`, and `collect`. That would be a permissible mechanism substitution only if the economic result were kept. It is not. The plan substitutes full-range liquidity accounting, a 1,000 share floor, and booked remainders. Those are the fullSpread results, not the wings results. Native-currency substitution is a fullSpread face rule (`Planner.sol` lines 248-256), not a wings rule in the files read. The plan's refusal of it does not create a wings match.

Plan choices, separated from required behavior: the plan itself labels file grouping, selector homes that preserve the gates, import execution return width, removal of the unnamed transition quotes, retention of `deployedReserve` as non-share data, fee-type re-keying, a second ledger file, and the `setOperator` disable revert as choices (plan lines 10-22). The 49 percent mint, liquidity share basis, 1,000 floor, usage fee, booked and unbooked split, sleeve removal, fail-closed exact-out, and the no-`getRate` surface are required by the plan, not labeled choices. Those required rows are what fail against wings.

This is not a partial yes. A partial plan is not a yes under the brief, and this plan is not partially accurate as a definition of wings behavior. It defines a different vault. The surface overlaps above do not define the listed economic results.

Observation, not the requested confirmation: current V3 production code follows the plan on the paths read. It does not match the identified wings vault. It does match the present fullSpread planner's economic results on the rows the plan copies, with named mechanism differences.

Static reading, not execution, shows the V3 planner swaps 49 percent inside the range (`Planner.sol` lines 374-380), issues liquidity shares and a 1,000 floor (`Math.sol` lines 115-126; `Repo.sol` lines 43-47), takes the fee from gross shares (`Math.sol` lines 130-138), books fees and claims (`Common.sol` lines 146-155 and 187-211), reports reserves as principal plus unpaid fees (`ReserveTarget.sol` lines 8-13), fail-closes exact-out (`OutExecuteTarget.sol` lines 10-15; `OutQueryTarget.sol` lines 7-9), and installs bundle, donate, compound, and import. The package name is `UniswapV3FullSpreadStandardExchangeVaultDFPkg.v4Accounting.v1` (`DFPkg.sol` lines 145-146). The bundle facet imports the fullSpread bundle interfaces (`BundleFacet.sol` lines 5-7). That is evidence the current V3 code was written toward the plan and toward fullSpread, not toward wings.

Against the present fullSpread source, the same observation: the live fullSpread planner uses the 49 percent interior swap, boundary no-swap, liquidity shares, 1,000 floor, booked exit claims, pass-through, donate and compound, and fail-closed exact-out (citations in the evidence table). The V3 plan keeps those results and refuses three fullSpread mechanisms: `unlockCallback`, native-currency WETH facing, and `ERC20Repo._mint(address(0), MIN_SHARES)` (`Execution.sol` line 239; plan disagreement on the floor write). The first two are V4 pool mechanisms. The floor-write refusal is required by the plan and is aimed at the same `balanceOf(address(0)) == 1,000` result, not at a different share amount. `quadraticInput` remains in fullSpread `Math.sol` at line 160; the planner `_size` that was read does not call it. The plan's "do not add `quadraticInput`" matches that live planner path. fullSpread has no import; the plan keeps import and the PRD labels that as not a V4 copy. Those differences explain why the plan can be a close definition of present fullSpread accounting and still not be an accurate definition of the supported V4 SE vault.

The gas citation is not the behavior verdict. The ceilings in plan step 10 match section 2 of the in-tree fullSpread implementation plan, line 24. They do not match section 2 of `docs/plans/UNISWAP_V4_FULLSPREAD_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_TEST_PLAN.md`, which starts at line 29 and states accounting quantities. The V3 plan names no path. That is a citation ambiguity in the plan's gas step.

Q2 is not confirmed because Q1 is not yes. The static observation that V3 code follows the plan is not a finding that the V3 vault was refactored to behave like the Uniswap V4 SE vault identified here.
