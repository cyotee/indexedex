### Required behavior versus declared choices

The plan explicitly distinguishes ordering and unspecified filenames from required behavior (`Plan:10-12`). Its raw-liquidity issuance, floor, fee deduction, no-sleeve policy, exact-out rejection, and removal of SY/rate surfaces are substantive requirements, not optional grouping decisions (`Plan:43,65-72,104-116`). These substantive requirements cause the Wings mismatch.

Declared plan choices include transition-selector removal beyond the expressly prohibited `quoteAssets`, retention or removal of `deployedReserve`, storage-file organization, preserving the historical fee-type key, disabled appointment behavior, facet grouping where not locked, import execution return width, and applying the gas bar to V3's additional import route (`Plan:18-24,40,61,102,115-118,125-131,155,193`). The comparison does not promote those choices into copied V4 requirements.

Import retention, import after existing supply, burning the empty NFT, new-vault-only scope, and removal of Standardized Yield are presented as owner locks in the required PRD (`PRD:189-221`). Their authority within that document is distinct from their suitability for this council's V4 SE parity question. I do not reopen or reverse those locks.

## Conclusions

**Q1 verdict: plan not accurate.**

The V4 vault used for this answer is **UniswapV4UpperLowerWingsStandardExchangeVault**, under `contracts/vaults/standard/exchange/protocols/uniswap/v4/upperLowerWings/`. The plan accurately identifies its own narrower task as satisfying a PRD that explicitly chooses the present V4 fullSpread family and excludes Wings.
