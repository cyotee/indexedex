## Conclusions

### Source observations

The plan does not merely omit a few Wings features. Its authority expressly rejects Wings as the template (`PRD:31`). The behaviors it marks required are the historical FullSpread economics: raw-liquidity shares, a 1,000-share floor, deposit-fee deduction, one position, no sleeve, principal-plus-fee reserves, owner/operator maintenance, no rate/SY surface, and fail-closed exact-out (`PLAN:43`, `PLAN:65-71`, `PLAN:104-105`, `PLAN:114`, `PLAN:141-164`). The supported Wings contracts implement materially different economics and surfaces, as cited in the evidence table.

The limited matches are a target-token exact-input deposit/share-in withdrawal subset, deadline-before-deposit-disable ordering, disabled-deposit rejection with withdrawal still available, and harvesting incumbent fees before subsequent share valuation (`PLAN:90-95`; `WINGS/UniswapV4UpperLowerWingsStandardExchangeVaultQuoteBase.sol:20-32`; `WINGS/UniswapV4UpperLowerWingsStandardExchangeVaultMoney.sol:43-46`, `WINGS/UniswapV4UpperLowerWingsStandardExchangeVaultMoney.sol:125-133`, `WINGS/UniswapV4UpperLowerWingsStandardExchangeVaultMoney.sol:147-161`). Those common properties do not make the differing issuance, exit, reserve, maintenance, quote, gate, and exact-out results equivalent.

The behaviors that do not match are: share issuance and genesis floor; usage-fee deduction; non-target one-token deposits; both-token deposits and exits; pass-through; raw-liquidity versus liquidation-value exits; treatment of pretransferred vault-held shares; one-position versus core-plus-wings inventory; no-sleeve versus oracle-sleeve reserves; booked-remainder maintenance versus automatic harvest/placement and permissionless rebalance; removed versus available rate/SY/transition quotes; fail-closed versus working exact-out; and locked-pool revert versus unlocked-context local inventory behavior. Each is tied to the cited plan and Wings lines above.

### Inference and verdicts

- **Q1 verdict: plan not accurate.** The V4 vault used is `UniswapV4UpperLowerWingsStandardExchangeVault` under `contracts/vaults/standard/exchange/protocols/uniswap/v4/upperLowerWings/`. The plan accurately describes a refactor toward the admitted historical FullSpread sources, but that is not the requested live V4 SE behavior. The mismatch is economic, not a demand for identical filenames or for a V3 `unlockCallback`.
- **Q2 verdict: not confirmed.** Because Q1 is no, the conditional confirmation is unavailable. This verdict does not assert that no code changed; it denies the requested confirmation of refactor-to-Wings behavior.

### Separate observations, not the requested Q2 confirmation

- **Does current V3 follow the plan?** Substantially yes for the named production behaviors inspected; no material plan/code contradiction was found. The V3 zero-address floor and raw-share formulas are at `V3/UniswapV3FullSpreadStandardExchangeVaultRepo.sol:43-48` and `V3/UniswapV3FullSpreadStandardExchangeVaultMath.sol:115-137`; shared route planning is at `V3/UniswapV3FullSpreadStandardExchangeVaultPlanner.sol:95-160`; bundles, maintenance, import, reserves, and exact-out are implemented at the V3 lines cited in the evidence table. Gas compliance and runtime equivalence remain unverified.
- **Does current V3 behavior match the identified V4 vault?** No. Current V3 implements the plan's historical FullSpread-style accounting, including fail-closed exact-out (`V3/UniswapV3FullSpreadStandardExchangeVaultOutExecuteTarget.sol:10-16`) and principal-plus-fee reserves (`V3/UniswapV3FullSpreadStandardExchangeVaultReserveTarget.sol:8-22`), whereas Wings executes exact-out (`WINGS/UniswapV4UpperLowerWingsStandardExchangeVaultOutTarget.sol:8-23`) and maintains target-token local sleeve reserves (`WINGS/UniswapV4UpperLowerWingsStandardExchangeVaultMoney.sol:50-54`). The other decisive differences are the Wings issuance, route, exit, maintenance, quote, and unlocked-context behaviors cited above.
