## Conclusions

**Q1 verdict: plan partially accurate.** The V4 vault used for the comparison is the **Uniswap V4 full-spread Standard Exchange vault** in `contracts/vaults/standard/exchange/protocols/uniswap/v4/fullSpread/` (package `UniswapV4FullSpreadStandardExchangeVaultDFPkg`), i.e. the family the plan's authority names as the behavior source (PRD line 31) and the family whose implementation plan section 2 the plan cites for gas (plan line 193, verified).

- **Accurate.** Every economic rule the plan states as *required* matches the V4 full-spread production code I read: the 1,000-share non-reverting genesis floor outside gross shares; issuance from raw liquidity against pre-add supply; the usage fee taken out of gross shares and clamped at 3e17 without an oracle write, with no fee on pass-through and no exit fee; the single exact-input 49% entry swap with a whole-input refund when the swap is unformable, unspent or zero-output; the boundary wanted-token add with no swap; the zero-active-liquidity refund; the proportional both-token deposit with per-token refunds; the one-token exit that burns caller shares, floors fee and booked claims, swaps the entire opposing claim after the planned liquidity removal and fails without burning when that claim cannot be spent; the both-token exit that pays both claims without swapping; the pass-through that mints nothing and takes no fee; the separate booked ledger that is not a reserve and not spendable by a user add; reserves that report deployed principal plus unpaid fees only; the maintenance rules (eligible input from booked + collected fees + directed donation, proportional for both tokens, 49% shape for one token, booking rather than refunding the remainder, a silent empty compound, booking stray custody only after a qualifying operation); the operator authorization model; the deadline-before-disable ordering; the fail-closed exact-out; and the retired yield/rate surface. The plan's gas citation is also accurate. The plan also covers the whole behavior list in the brief, not only the PRD's narrower "deposits, exits, dust, and share issuance".
- **Not accurate in full.** Two things keep it from being a faithful, complete definition of "behaves like the V4 SE vault":
  1. **One observable gate is defined differently from the V4 vault.** The plan instructs that `setOperator` and `setOperatorFor` revert `VaultDisabled` while the vault is disabled (plan lines 24, 92, 147, 149). The V4 vault does not gate appointment on disable at all: `setOperator` and `setOperatorFor` carry only `onlyLiveFeeToOwner` and never call `_disable` (`v4/fullSpread/...MoneyTarget.sol:122-131`). The plan labels this a choice, and its authority does not require it (V3-ACC-16 governs execution routes; PRD line 159), so it is a plan choice rather than required behavior - but a plan-faithful V3 still behaves differently from V4 on this gate, and "disable gates" is inside the brief's definition of "behave like".
  2. **One execution case is left unspecified, and the implemented V3 resolves it differently from V4.** The plan's pass-through rule (plan line 92 area, V3-ACC-14) says the route refunds unspent input and that `minAmountOut` binds the swap output, but it does not say what a zero-output quote does. V4 refunds the whole input and returns a zero output, reverting `AmountOutNotMet` only when `minAmountOut > 0` (`v4/fullSpread/...Planner.sol:120-130`, `...Execution.sol:42-95`). The implemented V3 instead reverts `ExchangeInNotAvailable` (`v3/...Planner.sol:281-301`, `...InBase.sol:37-50`), so a zero-output pass-through that V4 settles as a full refund cannot be executed on V3.
- **Framing difference, recorded as evidence rather than as a verdict input.** The plan's own question is PRD satisfaction (plan line 4) and its requirement-check table (plan line 209) maps PRD rows to steps, not V4 behaviors to steps. Read as a parity specification the plan is complete on economics; read as it is written it is a PRD-satisfaction plan whose parity content comes from the PRD.
