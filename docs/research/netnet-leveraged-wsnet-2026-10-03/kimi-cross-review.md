## 1. Source-verified corrections

**MiniMax — "one diamond cannot be lender and borrower on the same market" (§2, :27–35, :206) is technically false.** Blue's `Position` struct is one record holding `supplyShares`, `borrowShares`, **and** `collateral` (`lib/crane/contracts/external/morpho/blue/interfaces/IMorpho.sol:16–17`, getter `:304`). Nothing in `borrow` forbids a supplier (`Morpho.sol:235–266`). The genuine objections to mixing are *economic* (reflexive self-dealing, mixed cash-flow signs) — and the user's framing doesn't even require lender+borrower in one product (moderator's clarification). His "incoherence" conclusion overreaches; his own §2.2 point ("one address = one position per market") is precisely what makes the **pooled** vault coherent: one position ⇒ fungible equity ⇒ transferable shares. His §6 claim that multi-user requires per-user vaults or non-transferable shares (:84–86) collapses under the pooled design — per-user `setAuthorization` vaults (his Option A/§7) are a *different* product (user-owned router-style, TurboRouter-like), a legitimate alternative, not a necessity.

**MiniMax — W/H axes and zero-debt edge wrong.** His `W = (D/targetLTV) − V` (:66) is a *distance-to-target in value units* with flipped sign relative to withdrawable collateral; and "at zero debt, W = ∞" (:90) is wrong — withdrawable collateral at D=0 is **all of C**. Correct continuous forms (Astra :46, Grok :47–54, my original, moderator's hint agree): `W = max(C − D/(τp), 0)`, `H = max(τpC − D, 0) = τpW`; joint budget for combined withdrawal x and borrow y: `τp·x + y ≤ slack`; both are 0 at target while equity is large; `W=C, H=τpC` at zero debt — adding their values manufactures a second claim. MiniMax's H keyed to LLTV (:67) vs target is also inconsistent with the user's "additionally borrowable" provider, which should match the vault's enforced band (target), with LLTV only as the liquidation wall.

**MiniMax — APR arithmetic wrong by 100×.** "`0.0045 × (0.04/0.75) × 1095 = ~0.26%` annual" (:136): the correct value is **0.2628 as a fraction ≈ 26.3%/yr simple**, not 0.26%. Likewise "only at P > 1.4 does rebase APY exceed 12%" — at P=1.4 the same formula gives 262.8%. His directional caution (carry is premium-gated and can be zero below NAV) survives; his quantitative trap conclusion does not. My original made no APR claim; this is pure arithmetic correction. (Compounding on the index only raises the figure.)

**MiniMax — §5 headroom-NAV attack is partly strawman.** Nobody proposed headroom as *share* NAV; the user proposed two **bound valuations/providers**, which are legitimate as deliberately custom headroom-priced trading views or capacity reporters. The PRD's supply-only exclusion is a permission boundary, not a technical impossibility. His A0/donation arguments (:77–80) are valid *against* headroom-priced share issuance, which Astra/Grok/I all independently reject anyway.

My `ceil(b·A/T)` followed TurboRouter's simpler ratio (`TurboRouter.sol:317–320`), which omits offsets and doesn't accrue — Astra's correction (:17–21) is right; reuse `MorphoBalancesLib`, not TurboRouter, for valuation.

So permissionless third-party *deleveraging* of the pooled vault is free; this satisfies Astra's "deterministic risk-reducing maintenance without fresh inflows" (:74) for the repay/collateral-add direction only.

**Astra — stands; strongest on sequencing.** Verified this session: repay reduces debt **before** `onMorphoRepay` and pulls loan tokens after (`Morpho.sol:286–295`) ⇒ callback-funded repayment from released collateral needs no external flash loan; full shares-mode repay leaves borrowShares=0 ⇒ `_isHealthy` returns true without reading the oracle (`:516`) ⇒ full collateral withdrawal survives a reverting (divergence-guard) oracle; conversion can still independently fail. ## 2. Agreements (four-way, source-backed)

- One vault = one Blue position = one equity share; NAV is equity `E = V − D` (plus owned idle) at an explicitly chosen accounting price distinct from the liquidation oracle and from exit proceeds.
- W/H are **alternative, mutually exclusive capacity views** spending one slack (`τp·x+y≤slack`); never additive assets, never share NAV, never both consumed. Two *selectable* bound providers reporting W/S and H/S are acceptable reporting; a consumer summing them is the error.
- Pooled vault: debt remains the vault's; remaining shareholders bear it pro-rata; no per-user Morpho debt transfer exists.
- Only-USDG exit without conversion is not a close; complete USDG close **is** attainable with conversion using Morpho callbacks alone (TurboRouter unwind precedent, `TurboRouter.sol:235–294`).
- Oracle ≠ spot ≠ backing ≠ buyback: `clamp(0.9·TWAP, B, 5B)·index`, fail-closed, pausing borrows **and** liquidations (`LoopbackOracle.sol:52–76,22–27`); NAV-floor credit is not an executable floor.
- Rebase is premium-gated (0 at/below NAV; stale TWAP mints 0 rather than reverting — `Distributor.sol:85–98`); index moves only on **processed** rebases, ≤1 epoch per staking touch (`Staking.sol:129–150`; TurboRouter's settle/loop pattern `:264–275,302–308`).
- Encumbered majority supply is not a price floor: marginal sellers set price, the bid is capped/discounted/gated, collateral can be liquidated and sold into the same pool; concentration is systemic risk, not support.
- Current law: v0.36's Morpho option is supply-only (:172; D6); leverage is unselected new scope everywhere.

## 4. Strongest falsifiable design conditions (for any accepted variant)

1. Shares mint/burn only on pre-credit, accrual-first equity deltas; donations and third-party `repay`/`supplyCollateral(onBehalf=vault)` credited once, never minting shares (A0/K1 discipline, MorphoBlueSE PRD D22).
2. Every debt/collateral-changing route enforces post-op `LTV ≤ τ < LLTV` with τ below LLTV by more than one zero-rebase interest interval (Grok :104); `W`,`H` computed with virtual-offset debt and directed rounding, H additionally capped by live market cash (`Morpho.sol:259`).
3. Full-close path provable: repay-by-shares → zero-debt oracle bypass (`:516`) → withdraw all collateral → convert (taxed pool and/or InverseBond within its public cap) → user minima, atomic.
4. Hook/family consumption, if ever: equity rate only; documented behavior when the rate is 0 (current hook reverts, `ClaimLib.sol:100`); no W/H as rate assets; no double-binding one SE (`_nativeAt`).
5. Returns metrics computed per funded participation cohort; donations excluded; no encumbrance-derived price-floor claim in product copy.
