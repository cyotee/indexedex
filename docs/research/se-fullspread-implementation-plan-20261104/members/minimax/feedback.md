## Disposition

**requested-changes**

The draft is a strong, complete plan. It adopts the brief's locked readings, keeps all nineteen headings in order, copies the stage-one gas meter, names the temporary-hook facet from its factory file, cites the consumer index without pasting it, maps all thirty-nine FS-A rows and all forty-five inventory rows to named spec files, and states the one-token pretransfer reading accurately as my assumption A1, which I confirm. Three defects change what gets built and must be corrected before publication:

1. Section 7's query-facet bullet contradicts section 7's own later sentence and FS-OWN-07, so the installed manifest is ambiguous on the two family reserve getters.
2. Section 7's prohibition list names `poolManager` without the "family-only" qualifier that FS-OWN-01 and FS-OWN-02 require, which reads as forbidding the Crane Protocol Aware getter that FS-A01 requires to be callable.
3. Section 16 scopes the 64-byte migration to `previewExchangeIn` readers only, while FS-OWN-08 also requires one-word `exchangeIn` decoders to read the tuple; two production call sites in this repository assign that result to a single `uint256` and will not compile once the Crane interface changes.

Seven further rows in Concerns are implementability gaps rather than contradictions: the bundle routes' rejection errors and the unsupported-combination rule are never stated, the standalone per-call gas measurement has no owning file, the funding and authority step and the non-payable boundary are missing from the check order, the hook-matrix adapter fixture is never named, FS-A38 and inventory row 045 are mapped to different files, the file map adds two library files beyond FS-ARCH-01's list, and "reservations" is used three times without a definition. None of these requires the owner; all of them are plan-text corrections.

This feedback does not authorize implementation, does not replace the draft, and does not direct the coordinator to publish or not publish it.

## What was not adopted from others

I did not read peer responses or peer feedback, so this section records what I did not adopt, using the coordinator's own merge notes in the draft as the source for the other members' proposals. Nothing here is a criticism of a peer I have not read.

- **Astra's namespace spelling `fullspread`:** not adopted by the draft, and not adopted by me. The draft's `indexedex.vaults.standard.exchange.uniswap.v4.fullSpread` matches my submission and the family directory spelling.
- **Grok's extra `PkgInit` field `operableFacet` and the `basicVaultFacet` to `multiAssetBasicVaultFacet` rename:** not adopted by the draft, and not adopted by me. FS-FACET-01 names `basicVaultFacet`, and the appointment selectors belong on a facet that is already in the package.
- **Kimi's and Astra's placement of the FS-OWN-33 sizing inside `SwapProjection`:** not adopted by the draft; the sizing is in `Math` and `SwapProjection` holds the unbounded exact-input swap only. My submission proposed the opposite placement. I accept the draft's split, because it is the arrangement in which a pass-through cannot reach the liquidity-targeting shape by calling the swap library, which is what the brief's adopted reading requires.
- **My own `SwapProjection`-as-the-quote-engine choice:** not adopted by the draft, which uses `UniswapV4FullSpreadStandardExchangeVaultPlanner` as the one quote engine. I accept that name. The load-bearing part of my proposal is carried over into section 12: the stored Quoter, State View, and Reserves Lens are not called on any path, there is no step cap, and there is no `QuoteWorkLimit`.
- **My modifier file name `…OperableModifiers.sol`:** not adopted by the draft, which uses `…MaintenanceModifiers.sol` with `onlyLiveFeeToOwnerOrOperator`. I accept the name; the three-arm shape is what matters and it is correct in section 14.
- **My Crane directory `…/uniswap/v4/position/` for the `UniswapV4SinglePositionAware` pair:** not adopted by the draft, which puts both Crane pairs under `…/uniswap/v4/aware/`. I accept it as a name.
- **My `positionSalt()` getter name and my `uint256[2] booked` ledger layout:** adopted by the draft; no difference to record.
- **My assumption A1, the one-token pretransfer shortfall as a no-op success:** adopted by the draft and stated accurately in the merge notes and in section 9. I confirm it and I do not reopen it. The bundle case is treated as the atomic revert the 2026-11-03 F1 override requires, and that too is stated accurately.

I did not adopt, and did not ask the draft to adopt, anything that would reopen a locked decision: the gas ceilings and the meter, the unbounded entry and exit limits, the empty `Donation()` and `Compounded()` events, the three-arm live-`feeTo()` operator model, the disable predicate widened to every execution route, the FS-TEST-04 inventory marks, and the 64-byte preview return are all carried into the draft unchanged.
