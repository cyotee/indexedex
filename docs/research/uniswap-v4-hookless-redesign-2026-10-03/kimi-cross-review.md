2. **"Exits are already search-free" was overstated.** The redemption *swap* is sized (whole opposing entitlement), but idle `RedeemExactIn` quotes currently append `_maintenance` (`InQueryTarget.sol:150,184–186`). Grok's formulation is correct: making redemptions search-free requires removing maintenance from the quote/user path, not merely noting the swap is sized. My original under-stated a required change.
3. **Refund law.** My "exact refund `r_i`" framing conflicts with standing law: exact-in refunds nothing; dual operations pull the quoted used amount and refund nothing; pretransfer excess is never credited. Correct restatement: the dual join **pulls exactly** `C_i = ceil(m·B_i/S)` per leg (`used ≤ max`), so no refund event exists; under pretransfer, undeclared surplus stays unbooked donor-risk. Astra/Grok's "retain and book identified excess" is the consistent reading when the caller has already transferred more than `C_i`. Which interface semantic (pull-exact vs retain-and-book) is an owner decision, not a detail.
4. **Rank 2 demoted.** Grok §3's source-level analysis is right: inside one step the continuous alignment equation is at most quadratic, but the integer predicate is not that polynomial — net-input floor, `getNextSqrtPriceFromInput`, rounded amount deltas, two-floor own-LP fee accrual (`QuoteService.sol:134–139`), and placement rounding loss (`TransitionPlanner.sol:135–143`) are each integer deltas. A candidate plus one check is a *reject policy* with unproven liveness, not a sizing equation. My Rank 2 must not be presented as preserving single-token entry; it survives only as "evaluate one candidate exactly, revert on failure," with unknown revert frequency.
5. **Orbital opposite tokens not established.** My claim that Orbital autonomously supplies the conversion leg assumed it can source the nested SE's opposite token. The `Univ4Se` fixture seeds two SEs with *distinct* opposite tokens (`seOther0`/"RATE0", `seOther1`/"RATE1", `TestBase_UniswapV4Detf_Orbital_Univ4Se.sol:40–41,50–53`), so no single reserve asset covers both nested vaults. Orbital swapping into each SE's opposite token is mechanically search-free under pull-exact join semantics (residual simply stays buffered), but the ratio choice is an economic policy input, and undeployed residual may violate Orbital's own buffering invariants. Astra's "not a drop-in Orbital buffer" stands against my Rank 1's stronger wording.
