# What was not adopted from others

- I do not adopt the draft-attributed claim that selling an exiter's slice leaves remaining holders' underlying token components unchanged. Remaining LP positions can fill that sale. The allocation invariant must remain tied to its cutoff.
- I do not adopt an opposite-sign numerical row as a rebuttal to my closed positive witness when the inventory, mint numerator, cutoff, or execution machine differs. The draft appropriately notes that different machines can have different signs; it should attach those machine definitions to their numbers.
- I do not adopt a finite grid with zero positive outcomes as a universal safety proof. The vector equality, with its domain, is the supporting proof for the allocation claim.
- I do not adopt a broad identification of all fee-aware post-swap formulas with the gross executed-price formula. Net retained credit and gross retained credit are distinct even when both have negative examples.
- I do not independently endorse the other members' or coordinator's re-execution numbers merely because the draft reports them. Their artifacts were outside this advisory read. My support for the narrow negative answer rests on the exact derivations and closed ledgers from my original response.
- My original limits remain: price-free proportional acceptance prevents the demonstrated ownership distortion under its stated conditions; it is not a guarantee against all adverse LP trading. A retained-credit ideal-TWAP policy has not been exhaustively classified by my evidence.
