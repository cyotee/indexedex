## 5. Conservation and engineering analysis (inference, derived from verified code + owner rules)

- **One physical pool, two price coordinates:** NET-out and sNET-out debit the same BasicVaultRepo-tracked SY reserve while priced differently. Every debit must update the stored reserve once (`_updateReserve` post-balance-change), and SY redeemed to NET must cease to back the sNET leg's virtual balance in the same atomic transition — no double credit.
- **Cross-leg swap effect:** a NET-out swap changes the SY leg's inventory and both legs' rated books; quotes/previews for subsequent operations must read the post-swap state (existing one-snapshot rule, P:234).
- **Claim-to-cash and force claims:** mid-sequence interest/reward claims move receivable→held once (P:353 boundary preserved); third-party force claims (P:617) can interleave between quote and execution — require coherent snapshot + post-claim reserve sync. Non-SY reward tokens claimed in the funding sequence are forwarded to current `feeTo()` under non-blocking retry (P:716); a failed forwarding must not revert the swap nor be counted as funding.
- **Provenance:** principal-exit SY from HLP sub-reserve exits is not swap-funding inventory (A27 rewrite); contributed SY vs earned interest vs retained incentive receipts remain distinct (C12, P:53/840).
- **Finite liquidity vs virtual price (real blocker B1):** the zap-out *valuation* can quote an output the SY reserve cannot fund; after held-SY-then-claim, insufficiency must revert atomically (no principal fallback is selected). NET/sNET swap availability is thereby coupled to interest accrual rate — an intended property ("sell earned interest to grow principal"), but it must be specified and tested (availability/liveness matrix), not discovered in production.
- **Rebasing sNET (B6, carried):** SY redemption to NET vs sNET are different `getTokensOut` paths with rebase-aware normalization; verify deployed SY surface before freezing (prior-round Context7/Pendle finding: `previewRedeem` is officially "not audited for on-chain use"; `exchangeRate()` is asset-denominated — `IStandardizedYield.sol:101,142,153`).
