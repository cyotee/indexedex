## Challenges to peer proposals (verified against code/law)

1. **Unauthorized failure-semantics changes in "bounded defaults" — reject.**
   - MiniMax R3 (`minimax-original.md` §R3) puts in the default: try/catch around `_recomputeL2`/`_syncVaultReserves` (stale cache on failure) and **skipping the protocol-fee mint when the rate is dead** (`_maybeMintProtocolFee`, `O/Common.sol:899-915`). A skipped mint is a fee-semantics change under failure; exiting LPs during the outage dodge dilution. MiniMax itself flags it as OD-2 needing owner ruling — including it in the default contradicts that. Reject from default.
   - Grok item 5 (`grok-original.md`) proposes a **scaled-reserve fallback** (`newReserve = oldReserve × newInventory / oldInventory`) written into `MultiAssetBasicVaultRepo` during outages, plus radius checks on scaled values. That publishes fabricated rated data on the discovery surface and admits joins against it — a semantic change, exactly the "stale/scaled-reserve fallback" to challenge. Reject from default.
   - **Astra's default is the correct one** (`astra-original.md` §1, R4): preserve atomic fail-closed failure; complete rate-failure liveness is *not* achievable without changing preserved bookkeeping (`O/Common.sol:1362→472-480`, `:1366→459-469` are ungated). Recovery/caching/waiver variants are all owner decisions.
2. **MiniMax's "≤ 1 wei" drift bounds are unjustified.** Minted shares = `min_i floor(S·C_i/B_i)`; a 1-unit floor error in a rated term shifts the ratio by ~1/B_i and the mint by ~S/B_i wei — arbitrarily large in relative terms on small books (prior round: raw 1/2 vs rated 1/3). Replace with **exact raw-ratio equality** and a measured differential, not a fixed wei bound (`minimax-original.md` A2, §3 differential, A9 is fine on gas).
3. **"Rate-invariant zap mint" is overclaimed.** The rated swap legs (`O/ExitQuoteLib.sol:297-344`) move with the rate; post-swap inventory changes; therefore zap minted LP changes with rate through the swaps by design. Only the **terminal ratio step** becomes rate-free. Grok's acceptance bullet and MiniMax's ZapIn test must be qualified: with swap simulation outputs held fixed, the terminal mint equals the raw share ratio; end-to-end, rate moves legitimately change zap outcomes. Astra states this correctly (R2: "only terminal admission/inversion becomes inventory-domain").
4. **Raw partial invariant cannot reuse rated R — Astra is right; MiniMax/Grok under-gate.** `sphereNavShares` (`O/Math.sol:380-391`) prices partial issuance against rated reserves and rated `R`. MiniMax R1 swaps raw inventory into `_partialNavShares` denominators while keeping the rated radius — a new unit mix, not a fix. Grok item 3 fixes the ratio numerator but leaves NAV pricing rated. **Correction to my own original:** my R3 put partial-join conversion in the default; the NAV pricing rule is genuinely unspecified. Default scope = full-book joins, zap terminal mint, flexible full-book twins, exits (unchanged), conversions (unchanged). **Gated:** the partial-join pricing rule — either (a) keep rated sphere-NAV with unit-consistent rated contributions (status-quo semantics, fixing only the face/rated mix at `O/Common.sol:1162-1184`, `1147-1158`), or (b) an owner-specified raw partial rule. Do not implement a hybrid silently.
5. **Ceil used-share backing vs floor payouts.** Astra R2 (required inventory `ceil(m·B_i/S)`, verify actual contribution backs mint) is the conservative, correct direction. MiniMax's `used0 = minShares * rawShares[i] / supply (floor)` under-collects against the minted ratio and favors the joiner. Adopt: mint ratios floor; used/required amounts ceil; buffered-leg face pulls ceil through the inverse quote so delivered shares ≥ required; exit payouts floor (unchanged). Acceptance must include a backing inequality, not just preview parity.
6. **Effective loop bounds / inverse-quote support.** Raw share→face inversion needs `previewExchangeOut(token, se, shares)` support or bounded search (`O/ClaimLib.sol:250-276` pattern, ≤64 doublings; `_faceForClaim` `O/ExitQuoteLib.sol:347-374`, ≤8 probes). Astra's caution stands: not every SE supports exact-out inversion; unsupported ⇒ revert (existing law), not linear extrapolation. Keep iteration bounds unchanged; external-preview counts multiply by 3 legs — **measure gas**, never assert from the `O/Common.sol:1929-1931` comment.
