## 1. Corrections to my own round-3 original (evidence changed my view)

**C1 — My Q5 "D=0 ⇒ F*=0 ⇒ full deploy" was wrong.** The approved target is computed from T: `F* = T·p/(1+p)` (PZ-3, PRD §4 line 87), not `p·D_current`. With `D=0, F>0`: `T=F>0` ⇒ `F* = T·p/(1+p) > 0`, and placement deploys `T/(1+p)` — a normal fixed-point move, not "deploy everything." Astra's engineering item ("compute targets from T even when D=0") is correct; the literal `F=pD` and the fixed-point form coincide only at steady state, and the PRD's normative formula is the T-based one. My zero-deployed question collapses into a pure engineering rule (targets from T; no revert solely for D=0; define view denominators).

**C2 — My Q1 compensating-factor claim was imprecise in an important direction.** I said blocked sleeve deposits "earn no fees." The deposited *inventory* earns no swap fees while sleeve, but the minted *shares* immediately own a pro-rata claim on the whole book including deployed L — blocked depositors participate in total vault earnings like everyone else. This cuts both ways for the roundtrip question and reinforces the brief's tempering: a quote differential between branches is not proof of extractable profit. My Q1 stands as a launch-gate question, but with the earning-participation correction and with profit explicitly unproven (Astra Q5 and Grok's "critical subtlety" take the same position; see §3 on MiniMax).

**C3 — My Q2 recommendation (permit `minSharesOut=0` as documented opt-out, no protocol-side bound) is superseded.** `minSharesOut` is a share-*count* bound against the caller's own expectation; it is not independent fair-value or starting-price protection. If the pool state is manipulated before the call, preview, execution and the post-swap book all live at the manipulated state and `minSharesOut` can pass while the caller receives fairly-computed shares at an unfair price. And rejecting `0` alone is nearly meaningless (1 wei ≈ 0). Astra Q1 (protocol-level bounds independent of minShares) and Grok Q1 (mandatory finite `sqrtPriceLimitX96` from slot0 at call start + package-constant max impact, regardless of minShares; do not require `minSharesOut>0`, which breaks existing callers) are both stronger than my position. I adopt: **mandatory protocol impact bound on the composition swap, independent of user minShares; minSharesOut tightens, never disables.**
