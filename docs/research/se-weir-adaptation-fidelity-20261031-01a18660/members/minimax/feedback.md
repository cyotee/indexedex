## Disposition

**The draft states my original substantially accurately, and its headline framing is right.** It carries my C1 conclusion without softening it — that the PRD is a PairToken vault whose position manager happens to be a full-range Uniswap V4 position, that deleting the sibling sentence without replacing the inherited economics would leave the same design under a new name, and that the money rules must be respecified from Weir. The seven named items are dispositioned in the same direction as in my original: `maxZapBps` removed as an inherited package input, `maxZapTickMove` removed as the copied knob with the replacement-guard question left open, `previewZapCredit` removed, the booked no-share remainder removed in favour of Weir's refund and idle rules, `IncompleteConversion` removed, `donate` recorded as already locked by FS-OWN-09 with the contradiction referred to the owner and not voted away, and `compound` kept with the harvest-and-reinvest behaviour. The preserved-disagreement table renders my recorded positions correctly on the tick bound: remove the knob; if a guard is wanted, it should be a Weir-shaped price limit, authorized separately, with no number proposed.

**Six corrections are needed, and two material items are missing.** Four concern the `compound` row and the coverage table, where the draft records removals that my original does not contain and presents them as council-adopted; one concerns the `donate` row, where the owner is asked to decide on a contradiction without the case the locked basis requires; one is the draft's own fingerprint line. The two omissions are the issuance rule and the compound-before-every-action ordering, both of which my original classified as material carry-overs and neither of which appears anywhere in the draft. My recorded preference on `donate` and my position on the price-limit form are preserved in the draft as they stand; I am not asking the coordinator to change either, and I do not resolve any of the three preserved splits here.

## Concerns

Each item names the draft section and the exact correction.

**1. Proposed text → table, row `compound`.** The clause "Remove the PairToken compound swap, quarter-cap, and no-fee rule." does not appear in my original and is not traceable to it. My original's `compound` disposition was **Keep** on the function — Weir has the operation and the standard agrees with it, so no authorization case was needed — and it recorded exactly two divergences to be stated rather than removed: the fee-attribution model, which the PRD takes from S1-ACC-06 rather than Weir's treasury cut, and the dropped treasury performance fee, which I called defensible because the standard has its own fee surface (`vaultFeeTypeIds`, `vaultConfig`, S1-ACC-06 attribution) but which must be recorded as a deliberate divergence with its consequences stated (no treasury, no `setTreasury`, no `setPerfFee`, a changed compound inventory formula). Correction: keep "Weir has the operation. Keep the harvest-and-reinvest behavior," and replace the three-item clause either with an explicit note that the compound row carries no member's recorded removal, or with each removal item cited to the requirement ID it comes from and to the member who proposed it. "No-fee rule" has two readings and the draft must pick one: if it means the PRD's "no vault-level performance fee" (PRD line 208, FS-A05 line 348), removing it reinstates Weir's treasury cut and reverses my recorded position on that sub-item, which then needs its own case — who receives the fee, which address, which cap; if it means a rule that PairToken's compound takes no fee, that rule is still not in my original and needs a source before it can be presented as adopted.

**2. Proposed text → table, row `donate`; and Preserved disagreements → table, row `donate versus Weir`.** The disposition is right and I do not ask for it to change. What is missing is the case. The locked basis in `PAIRTOKEN_FUNCTIONS_FOR_REVIEW.md` admits a PairToken aspect "only if the owner explicitly authorizes it after a stated case," and the draft frames the open question as "whether the owner keeps `donate` after seeing that contradiction" — which asks the owner to decide on a contradiction alone. Correction: carry the stated case in the record, or cite where it lives, in three parts: the Weir behaviour it replaces (WI-24 — receipts arriving without a route stay unassigned and unbooked, never creating shares, liquidity, idle, a preview entry, or a performance fee); the harm if it is absent (under FS-OWN-07 a raw transfer silently raises the reported `reserves()` with no share issued and no preview change, and a third party has no route at all to add backing to a vault whose only routes are issuance and redemption); and the cost of keeping it as currently specified (a booked backing class that is neither Weir's idle nor an unassigned receipt, with no stated redemption or preview treatment, so it would sit in `reserves()` undistributed). My own recorded preference was to drop it and leave WI-24's raw-transfer semantics as the rule; that preference is mine, it does not resolve the split, and the draft preserves it accurately as it stands.

**3. Proposed text → table, row `Booked no-share remainder`.** "Remove as an entrant donation. Weir refunds unused input. Idle dust from fees is a different rule." loses the rule being adopted, and "Idle dust from fees is a different rule" is not quite the assertion that matters. Correction: state it as — remove the PairToken booking of the addition remainder; adopt Weir's rule, under which the unspent input is refunded to the payer, idle arises only from compound, and idle is (i) excluded from the issuance denominator, (ii) claimable proportionally at withdrawal, and (iii) computed by Weir's compound inventory formula with the two-unit cushion; and the only channel for the unspent input is the already-locked `(amountOut, amountRefunded)` return of FS-OWN-08, so no second booked-remainder class may exist. Separately, my original recorded a conditional case that the draft's flat "Remove" drops: if the owner wants a booked remainder instead of a refund, it must be excluded from the issuance denominator and given a stated preview and redemption treatment, or it dilutes existing holders. Correction: add that case to the preserved-disagreements table, or qualify the row so the case is not lost.

Correction: split the row so that the removals my original does support (`maxZapBps` as an inherited package input, `previewZapCredit`, `IncompleteConversion`, the booked-remainder booking) are recorded as council-adopted, and so that `compound` is recorded as **kept** and `donate` as **already locked with the contradiction referred to the owner**; and remove the "All four" attribution from the compound row's three sub-removals until each is sourced per Concern 1.
