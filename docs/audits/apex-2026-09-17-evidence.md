## Integration notes

This section is the R12.6 integrator handoff. It does not authorize deployment, migration, registry disablement, or a claim that live instances were repaired.

### Contract-only pretransfer

Public `pretransferred=true` / `prepaid=true` / `burnFromInternalBalance=true` is for integrating contracts only. A code-less EOA reverts `EOAPretransferNotAllowed()` (`0xb18c67ea`) before any credit. Constructor-time callers also reject. Deployed wallets and EIP-7702 delegated accounts pass the bytecode check; that is the D12 `UNSUPPORTED_USE` residual, not a defect. Atomic transfer-then-call (`AtomicPretransferCaller`) is the supported way to consume unbooked credit in one transaction. Staged or partial consumption has no ownership or exclusivity. The next contract caller with a declared amount within unbooked credit can consume it. Booked holder inventory is never that credit.

D32 surfaces keep their existing hard public-pretransfer reject (`TransferDeltaInsufficient(..., 0)` on the Aave loop; `UnsupportedPoolPretransfer()` on Balancer native-BPT packages). Do not enable public pretransfer there. False-flag pull remains.

Selectors: `exchangeIn` `0x05562cc8`, `exchangeOut` `0x612d4427`. Crane In/Out NatSpec and IndexedEx InMulti/OutMulti / `IDetf` / `IUniswapV4Detf` state the same rule.

### Changed refund semantics

Exact-input pretransfer requires unbooked `available >= amountIn`, credits exactly `amountIn`, and refunds nothing. Excess unbooked is not this operation's input. Exact-input pull requires measured `actualIn == amountIn` or reverts `TransferDeltaInsufficient`.

Exact-output pretransfer credits `min(unbooked, maxAmountIn)` and refunds only `credit - used` to `msg.sender`. Amounts above `maxAmountIn` are never refunded. False-flag exact-output quotes `used`, pulls exactly `used`, and refunds nothing. Dual-exit burns `sharesToBurn`, not `maxAmountIn`. V3/V4 token exact-out no longer pull `quotedIn + quotedIn/1000 + 1` or `maxAmountIn`.

Booked inventory is never paid as a refund. Leftover after a successful credit is booked or sleeved per family R14 prechecks. Protocol residual is not paid to the fee recipient. `_absorbDustToFeeTo` has no in-scope refund-path callers.
