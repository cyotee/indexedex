## 2. Three invariant campaigns cannot start (R11) — RESOLVED 2026-09-20

**Required.** A 256-run, depth-64 stateful campaign for every D16 family.

**Recorded on 2026-09-18.** Campaigns for MixedBuffer MultiVault Stable DETF, MixedBufferMultiVaultStable and CommonBufferMultiVaultWeighted reverted in `setUp` with `ReentrancyGuardReentrantCall()` (`remaining-stdinvariant-256.log`, `apex-r11-detf-remaining-invariant.log`).

**Verified 2026-09-20.** All three campaigns pass on the current tree with their file-level 256 × 64 pins: `MixedBufferMultiVaultStableDetf_Invariant.t.sol` `invariant_inSucceeded` 256 runs / 16,384 calls; `CommonBufferMultiVaultWeightedPool.invariant.t.sol` 3/3 invariants at 256 / 16,384; `MixedBufferMultiVaultStablePool.invariant.t.sol` at 256 / 16,384 (`review-20260920/r11-nested-lock-*.summary.log`). They also ran green inside the full hermetic run 3.

**Actual root cause (corrected 2026-09-20).** The SE legs in all three fixtures are Aerodrome Standard Exchange vaults (`TestBase_CommonBufferMultiVaultWeightedPool.sol:129`, `TestBase_MixedBufferMultiVaultStableDetf.sol:236`); no Balancer vault is nested inside another. The re-entry was the pool hook calling back into its own Vault. At HEAD, `onAfterInitialize` in `CommonBufferMultiVaultWeightedPoolHookTarget.sol` and `MixedBufferMultiVaultStablePoolHookTarget.sol` called `_depositPhysicalBuffer(...)` to fold the initial physical buffer into the SE legs. That fold reaches `Vault.sendTo` / `addLiquidity`, which are `nonReentrant`, while `Vault.initialize` (`VaultExtension.sol:186`, also `nonReentrant`) is still on the stack, so the transient guard reverts. At HEAD the fold sat inside `try this.__reconcileAttempt(...) catch {}` and the revert was swallowed, so the fold never actually executed and initialization appeared to succeed. D37 removed the catches on 2026-09-18 and the revert surfaced in `setUp`. The executor then removed the fold from `onAfterInitialize` (current code comments: "Vault.initialize is nonReentrant. sendTo / addLiquidity / removeLiquidity share that guard, so the leftover-buffer SE fold cannot run here. Unbalanced add and afterSwap still fold physical buffer."). Behavior is unchanged from HEAD in practice, because the HEAD fold was dead code; it is now explicit instead of silently caught.

**Owner policy (2026-09-20), kept but re-scoped.** A Standard Exchange leg that is itself a Balancer-hosted vault on the same Vault would re-enter the Vault from inside a pool hook and is unsupported; such stacks are not tested and are recorded `INCOMPATIBLE` with the limitation named. That policy did not apply to these three failures, whose legs are Aerodrome SEs.

**Remaining.** None. The plan's R11.2 / R11.3 rows now include these three families in `acceptance.json`.

## 7. Letter-of-the-plan deviations with no economic effect

- Rocket Pool and EtherFi run the D31 sweep after `_mintWithUsageFee` rather than before. The share count is fixed from the pre-credit snapshot, so ordering does not change entitlements.
- EtherFi reads its boolean capacity once per operation instead of between the booked and caller legs. The answer cannot change within a transaction.
- On ERC-4626, Morpho and FullSpread exact-out, the `used > credit` check runs before the bytecode guard, so a short-funded EOA sees `TransferDeltaInsufficient` instead of `EOAPretransferNotAllowed`. Both are rejections with no state change; R13.2's exact-error assertion holds only for the funded case.

**To close.** Optional reordering if the owner wants the letter of D31 and R13.2 exactly; no security change.

### Item 5 elaboration (F2 and F7): which hooks have a configured rate provider

The owner's rule (apply the rate for swap quotes, use raw share balances for liquidity operations) is how the weighted, curve-quad, Balancer-quad and orbital families already work: their `PkgArgs` carry a `rateProviders` array per leg, their Repo stores it, and their claim libraries quote a buffered leg as `heldShares * rate / 1e18` when a provider is set (`getRateFailClosed`, D41 / D48), falling back to a full-balance `previewExchangeIn(se, heldShares, face)` only when the provider is `address(0)`.

The single-CP hook (`IUniswapV4SingleStandardExchangeBufferConstantProductHookPackage.PkgArgs`: poolManager, feeOracle, standardExchange, pairToken, rawToken, decimals, ownerOnlyLiquidity, owner) and the dual hook (`PkgArgs`: poolManager, feeOracle, standardExchange0, token0, standardExchange1, token1) have no rate-provider field, no Repo slot for one and no rate path in `ClaimLib._claimOf`, `_seClaim()` or `_previewSeClaimOfBal()`. They always value the SE leg with the full-balance exit quote. That is why F2 (the loop's exit quote is a liquidity check) and F7 (a Balancer pool SE cannot quote a full-supply exit) surface only on the single-CP rows, and why the same SEs are DEFERRED (M13) rather than tested on the dual hook.

So the question behind item 5 is not how to apply a rate but where the single-CP and dual hooks would get one. The options are: (a) add a `rateProvider` (single) / `rateProviders[2]` (dual) field to those two `PkgArgs`, store it in the Repo, and route the swap-side claim quote through it as the other four families do, keeping liquidity operations on raw SE share balances; this changes the package init encoding of two launched packages (no proxy selector change) and needs the launch scripts and TestBases updated; or (b) record the loop and the six Balancer pool SEs as unsupported on the single-CP and dual hooks and leave those packages rate-less. A decision on (a) or (b) is what closes F2 and F7.
