## Assumptions

- The Solidity destructuring rule cited from the public documentation applies to the compiler this repository uses: the left-hand side of a tuple assignment must present as many slots as the right-hand side returns, and a trailing comma after the last named slot is a blank slot rather than a shorter tuple. Under that rule the `(uint256 x,) =` form is a two-slot destructuring that consumes the first returned word and discards the second, and it is arity-compatible with a two-value typed return.
- The widened interface in `lib/crane/contracts/interfaces/IStandardExchangeIn.sol` is the declaration the two named assignments compile against, because `contracts/interfaces/IStandardExchangeIn.sol` re-exports it and both files call through that alias or through `@crane/...` directly.
