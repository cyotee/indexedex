**support-with-reservations.**

I support the draft's verdict, all four findings, the task/policy/acceptance dispositions and the adjudication of competing claims. Concretely:

- The verdict "The corrected plan is not fully and correctly executed" matches my own original's conclusion, and the draft's framing — two medium source findings, one low presentation finding, and a documentation gap — is the same shape I reached, arrived at independently for the documentation gap and by trace for the rest.
- **F1 (bootstrap leftovers stranded)** is correct, and I concur it is a real defect my original missed. My own source trace confirms the mechanism the draft describes: after `bootstrapMigrationReserve` selects less than the booked donations, `UniswapV4DetfMigrationAllocationTarget.sol:340-361` books the post-join balance delta, leaving the unspent units in `donationInventory`; `bootstrapMigrationReserve` is then closed by `isReserveLive`; `UniswapV4DetfMigrationPolicy.sol:29-38` sets the sweep floor to `balanceOf − residualInventory`, so `UniswapV4DetfMigrationWeightedHookTarget.sol:327-377`'s live donation path releases only the freshly donated amount and leaves the genesis remainder below the floor; `UniswapV4DetfMigrationRecoveryTarget.sol:441-468` spends separately booked recovery inventory. No route moves the remainder. The draft's severity ("Medium", "availability/accounting-transition defect, not demonstrated theft") and its evidence class ("current-source control/data flow plus an existing permitted test setup", "No new reproduction was executed") are appropriate.
- **F2 (retained native QA lifecycle cannot satisfy activation)** is correctly bounded: the draft states that the production activation guard is doing the correct thing and that the evidence is reachability analysis, not an observed revert. I did not read `S/MigrationCoreLocalQA.s.sol` line-by-line in my pass — my original recorded it as surface-extraction only — so I neither contest nor add to it.
- **F3 (owner-first bond metadata reports closed rewards as claimable)** is correct on the mechanism I traced independently: `M/bondNft/UniswapV4DetfMigrationBondMetadataTarget.sol:30-32` sets `rewardsClosed` only from `migrationPresentation`, so an ordinary purchased bond in the migration instance reports rewards as claimable while `M/bondNft/UniswapV4DetfMigrationBondNFTVaultTarget.sol:73-78` gates every reward claim on activation. The draft's remediation direction — derive reward availability from parent activation for every bond in this instance, independently of principal vesting style, while preserving ordinary-package rendering — is the right shape.
- **F4 (named completion documents)** is correctly adjudicated, and the draft is right to place my F1/F3/F5 into it with narrower severity.
- The **"Disagreement and findings not adopted"** section is accurate in every item I can check, including the two corrections against my own original (§ below), and it explicitly records my write-tool deviation as a deviation rather than as compliance. That is the right handling.
