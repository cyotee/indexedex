## Conclusions

1. The identity rejections are **not** a product defect in the sense of "the code is wrong". They are the plan's and the PRD's anticipated outcome, correctly recorded. `UniswapV4SeBufferHookLegLib.pairInVaultTokens` accepts a leg only when the token is in the SE's `vaultTokens()` or when the leg vault answers `asset()` with a different non-zero address. The FullSpread vault and the PairToken vault both initialize `vaultTokens()` from their faces and neither installs `asset()`, so `token == se` is rejected. Plan line 225 and PRD section 2.1 forbid adding `asset()` and forbid relabelling the rejected bind, so the rows are required to stay red. The smallest resolution for those failures is **no product-code change** plus a **test-record change** that keeps the required non-pass.
2. The one failure that is a genuine product gap is the disabled buffered withdrawal. The vault's disable predicate is correct as specified, but the composed hook path performs a share **mint** (the residual re-buffer) after the redemption, and FS-A38 requires the buffered withdrawal to succeed while disabled. This is a product-code change the PRD and the plan already require.
3. The supplied gas figures for the failing dual tests are not route measurements. Two of them (about 1.75e10 and 1.74e10) are harness cost from an in-test state revert and redeployment of the PairToken identity record; the rest are the cost of aborting at `_requireIdentity()`. The dual composed-route gas for FS-A31 is **unmeasured**, and the admitted matrix file already records it correctly as `coldWholeTransactionGas: null`. That null must survive into the resolution.
4. The evidence record is accurate about the identity gate but under-reports the buffer suite: the dual unwrap failure and the four inherited identity failures in the buffer suite's unwrap contracts are not named in the blocked-test lists, and the per-row matrix has no entry for the buffer suite at all.
5. One product decision the admitted documents do not make: **which requirement, in which family's source, carries the change that would let the canonical identity bind be accepted** — the PRD calls an `asset()` requirement "a separate requirement" and forbids implementing it here, the plan forbids adding `asset()` and forbids substituting a raw leg, and neither document names an owner, document or family for that change. I name it and do not choose it.
