## Question

Does the published vault specification, and any implementation it has, meet a fee-exposed reserve, a deposit of either token, a single-token withdrawal, and a one-share single-token quote? Report gaps and failures.

## Proposed text

No. We are not sure, and the written specification does not meet the four needs as a set. There is no implementation, so none of the four can be confirmed in code. The specification itself fails the either-token deposit. The other three are specified, with limits that must not be read as a clean pass.

This is not implementation authorization. Fix directions are advice. The specification was not edited.

### Finding 1. Either-token deposit is refused

- Location: `docs/research/v4-own-vault-spec-20261027-01a177b3/REPORT.md` lines 133-137 and 253. Coordinator re-read. "No vault-initiated swap." A zero amount is allowed only when the tick already leaves that side unused. A zero `uReceived` reverts. Line 253 says the vault does not swap on entry.
- Requirement: the vault can accept a deposit of either token.
- Impact: a caller who holds only one pool token cannot deposit while the tick uses both sides. Out of range, the unused token alone still yields no liquidity and reverts. The depositor cannot choose either token. Kimi and MiniMax call this partial because one side works when the tick already makes the other unused. That exception does not meet the need.
- Fix direction: add a separate entry that accepts either token, swaps only the amount required to add liquidity, and still mints with `floor(uReceived * (S + 10^d) / (U_before + 1))`. The swap changes `uReceived`. It must not enter the denominator, and it must not be described as closing the prior extraction result. Caller bounds must be able to revert the whole call. This direction is not an authorization to implement it.

Astra and Grok grade this failed. Kimi and MiniMax grade it partial. The failed grade is the one adopted.

### Finding 2. No implementation exists

- Location: repository search reported by all four members. No contract, facet, package, or test implements this specification. Wings and DETF surfaces are not this vault.
- Requirement: all four needs, as a product that can be used.
- Impact: nothing can be deposited, withdrawn, or quoted today. Specification grades are not code grades.
- Fix direction: none in this review. Implementation was not requested and is not authorized.

### Finding 3. Fee exposure is a side claim, and the liability sum is not an on-chain total

- Location: REPORT.md lines 44, 90-129, especially `claimFees` at line 125 and `liability_i` in the terms table. MiniMax's response.
- Requirement: a reserve exposed to trading fees.
- Impact: the position does earn fees, and holders can claim them in kind. The share redemption and `getRate` do not include those fees. Astra, Kimi, and Grok treat that as meeting the words, because the position is on the fee path. MiniMax treats it as partial, because the exposure is a separate two-currency claim and the liability definition sums every holder. That sum is not a stored total. An implementer who substitutes `floor(S * feePerShare / Q)` can overstate liability and drive remainder negative. That can make `claimFees` revert. The fee path is specified. It is not yet a payable on-chain rule.
- Fix direction: store the aggregate unsettled liability, or define remainder from escrow minus a stored claim total that settle updates. Do not require a loop over holders. Say in the quote section that `getRate` excludes fee escrow. Do not silently fold fees into the share.

The adopted grade is partial. The stronger reading "fees are inside the redeemable share" is not what the user wrote, and it is not adopted as a failure. The uncomputable liability is a failure of the written accounting, not of the idea that the position earns fees.

### Finding 4. The quote and the withdrawal are specified, and neither is built

- Location: REPORT.md sections 5 and 7. `getRate` at line 179. Exit at lines 153-155.
- Requirement: withdraw into one token, and quote one share into one token.
- Impact: the specification does both for the share's liquidity slice. The sale can revert. The last burn can leave residual liquidity. The quote excludes fee escrow and can revert on an unsupported hook or an unquotable fee. MiniMax adds that the repository's V4 quoter and StateView do not compose a removal-plus-sale preview, and `getRate` must not write. That is an implementation gap, not a missing equation. It does not change the specification grade. It does mean "we can quote" is false until that preview exists.
- Fix direction: when implementation is authorized, the quote must simulate removal first and then the sale, without an unlock that persists state. Do not treat `getRate` as total holder value.
