## Review findings

### F-01 — The "no compound on deposit or withdrawal" lock is not tied to the PRD sentences it removes

- **Location:** B-02, the bullet "`compound()` is restricted to the owner or an operator, using the existing `onlyOwnerOrOperator` guard. Any other caller reverts. A deposit, a withdrawal, and `exchangeIn` do not call `compound()`." Its PRD citation line names only FS-OWN-14, FS-OWN-12, FS-OWN-15, FS-MONEY-01 and FS-OWN-10.
- **Why two readers diverge:** the admitted PRD still says, in FS-MONEY-01, "`unlockCallback` calls `_compound` before `_deposit` and before `_withdraw`", and its acceptance rows FS-A11 ("Compound harvests and reinvests before deposit and before withdrawal") and FS-A33 ("Borrowed execution uses the same compound and settlement as the locked path from the same economic state") assert the same behavior. FS-A11 additionally asserts "Withdrawal includes pro-rata idle as in `_withdraw`", and FS-MONEY-01 still says "`_withdraw` removes liquidity in proportion to shares and pays pro-rata idle". A PRD writer who applies only the cited FS-OWN-14/FS-OWN-12 replacements leaves a PRD that harvests fees on deposit and withdrawal, pays pro-rata idle, and emits `Compounded()` on a user deposit. An implementor reading the rewritten PRD and the blockers file would have to choose between harvesting and not harvesting, which changes who owns the unpaid fees at the moment of a deposit and changes whether `Compounded()` can appear in a deposit transaction.
- **Fix direction (advice):** extend B-02's PRD citation line to name every sentence and acceptance row the lock replaces — FS-MONEY-01's compound-before-deposit and pro-rata-idle sentences, FS-A11 and FS-A33 — and state in one sentence what the deposit and withdrawal paths do instead (no harvest, no idle payment, fees left unpaid in the position). That keeps the single PRD pass to one edit and leaves the implementor no choice.

### F-02 — B-07 makes two selectors callable that FS-A01 asserts are absent, and FS-A01 is not cited

- **Location:** B-07: "This vault's package takes that facet as a package input and uses it to initialize the vault instances. The getters on that facet are externally callable on this vault. Do not implement a family `poolManager()` selector." and "The position getters are a second Crane pair, `UniswapV4SinglePositionAware` … It is a package input on this vault, and its getters are externally callable. Do not put a family-only `poolKey()` selector on the full-spread query facet." Its PRD citation line names FS-OWN-01, FS-OWN-18 and FS-OWN-16 only.
- **Why two readers diverge:** FS-A01 in the admitted PRD asserts that "`poolManager()`, family-only `poolKey()`, `rateAsset()`, `getRate()`, `basket()`, and every Standardized Yield selector are absent" from the reviewed selector manifest, and the FS-FACET-01 `queryFacet` row says "Do not install `poolManager()` (FS-OWN-01), a family-only `poolKey()` (FS-OWN-02)". After B-07 the diamond exposes `poolManager()` through the Crane Protocol Aware facet and `poolKey()` through the Crane position facet; what the lock forbids is a *full-spread-family-only* implementation of those selectors, not the selectors themselves. An implementor who follows FS-A01 writes a test asserting both selectors are absent and fails it; an implementor who follows B-07 installs them. The distinction the owner actually drew (standard pair in Crane, not a family query) is also the distinction a PRD needs to state, and it cannot be copied from B-07 as it stands.
- **Fix direction (advice):** have B-07 cite FS-A01 and the FS-FACET-01 `queryFacet` row, and carry one sentence the PRD can reuse: the selectors are present and are served by the Crane Protocol Aware and Single Position pairs, and what is absent is a full-spread-family-only implementation of them. Then FS-A01 becomes a sourcing assertion ("served by the Crane pair, not by a full-spread facet") instead of an absence assertion.

### F-03 — The one-token swap has no sizing rule, and "the position's ratio" is not a defined quantity

- **Location:** "Donation and compound booking", first bullet: "One token: swap toward the position's ratio, as a one-sided user deposit does. The remainder must be in the token being processed. Book that remainder." Also B-05: "A one-token underlying donation swaps its own tokens into the position's ratio and deploys that liquidity."
- **Why two readers diverge:** three different swap sizes are defensible from this text. (a) An exact-ratio swap of the amount needed to complete the pair. (b) The admitted PRD's own rule, FS-OWN-33: "A one-sided share mint swaps about half of the excess through the pool so the deposit matches the pool ratio" — a different quantity, and FS-OWN-33 is not cited as replaced anywhere in the file. (c) Any interpolation between the two. The swap size sets the deposited liquidity, the refunded remainder, the pool fee paid, and which token ends up holding the booked remainder, so two implementors produce materially different donation and compound outcomes. "The position's ratio" is also not tied to a source: on a constant-product pool the ratio implied by `getLiquidityForAmounts` for the stored ticks and the pool's spot price coincide, but FS-OWN-22 accepts any initialized hookless pool, and on a stable or weighted pool they do not. The second sentence is a constraint on the outcome ("the remainder must be in the token being processed") with no rule that produces it; whether it holds depends entirely on the swap sizing and on rounding direction, neither of which the file states.
- **Fix direction (advice):** state the sizing rule in one sentence (how much of the processed token is swapped, and against which ratio source — the pool price, or the amount ratio implied by the stored ticks), and state the rounding convention that keeps the leftover in the processed token. Then decide explicitly whether FS-OWN-33's "about half of the excess" wording survives for user deposits, is superseded for donations and compounds, or applies to both.

### F-04 — The owner value behind `onlyOwnerOrOperator` is a snapshot, and the zero case is unspecified

- **Location:** B-02: "When a new proxy is initialized, set its owner to the address returned by `feeTo()` on the Vault Fee Oracle. That is the owner `onlyOwnerOrOperator` uses." Repeated in the "Already locked" summary. B-05 locks `donate` to the same guard.
- **Why two readers diverge:** the existing Crane guard resolves the owner from stored state, not from a live call — `OperableModifiers.onlyOwnerOrOperator` delegates to `OperableRepo._onlyOwnerOrOperator()`, and the owner check goes through `MultiStepOwnableRepo` (`lib/crane/contracts/access/operable/OperableModifiers.sol`). So "set its owner to `feeTo()`" is a value written at initialization. The file does not say whether the stored owner must follow later `feeTo()` changes (the oracle's fee recipient can rotate), so one implementor writes the address once at init and another resolves `feeTo()` on every call, and the two differ in who may call `compound()` and `donate()` after a rotation. The file also does not say what happens when `feeTo()` returns `address(0)`: the stored owner would be the zero address, and `msg.sender` can never be `address(0)`, so `compound()` and `donate()` would be unreachable for everyone — a state that an implementor may well "fix" by substituting the deployer or the registry, which is an authority decision the owner has not made.
- **Fix direction (advice):** say in one sentence whether the owner is a stored value written at initialization or a live read of the oracle, and say what the owner is when `feeTo()` is `address(0)` (for example: the registry or deployer as a fallback, or `compound()`/`donate()` deliberately disabled). The fee mint in B-01 already skips a zero `feeTo`, so the two should be stated together.

### F-05 — The `donate` pretransfer input has no named home in the ABI

- **Location:** "Donation and compound booking", first sentence: "`donate()` also compounds. It uses tokens already transferred when `pretransferred` is set, and pulls the directed amount otherwise." Compare B-05 ("`donate` is `onlyOwnerOrOperator` … It reverts when `totalSupply` is zero") and B-13 (the pretransfer refund rules).
- **Why two readers diverge:** the booking rule requires `donate` to distinguish a pretransferred call from a pulled call, but nothing in the file says where that flag comes from. The admitted function review fixes the route at "`donate(token, amount)` stays" with no flag, and B-12 locks the paired-event ABI but is silent on `donate`'s parameters. The repository already contains both shapes — `contracts/interfaces/detf/IDetf.sol` declares `donate(IERC20 token, uint256 amount, bool pretransferred)`, while the PairToken stage-one PRD declares `donate(address token, uint256 amount)`. An implementor either adds a `pretransferred` bool (changing the selector, the interface, and the PRD's function list) or reuses some other mechanism to learn that the tokens are already held. Those are different ABIs and different refund behaviors under the B-13 rules, which distinguish "the token the caller provided" and the "unbooked held balance".
- **Fix direction (advice):** name the `donate` signature the PRD should carry, including the pretransfer flag and its position, or state that the flag arrives on the shared Standard Exchange interface and therefore does not change the family selector list. One line here removes an ABI decision from the implementor.

### F-06 — The batch-swap sentence is not scoped to a route and reads against FS-A22

- **Location:** B-02, final paragraph: "This vault can be included in a batch swap with other Uniswap V4 pools. That is not a revert. The earlier exclusion is removed. The borrowed-unlock path stays."
- **Why two readers diverge:** the admitted PRD's FS-UNLOCK-01 and FS-A22 say that on an already-unlocked manager only canonical `exchangeIn` may proceed and that "`exchangeBundleInForSingleOut`, `exchangeBundleOut`, `donate`, and `compound` revert `ManagerAlreadyUnlocked` while the manager is unlocked". A batch swap holds the manager's unlock, so "This vault can be included in a batch swap … That is not a revert" is true for the exact-input route and false for the four routes FS-A22 names. Copied into the PRD as written, it either contradicts FS-A22 or silently reopens a rejection the owner did not reopen. "The earlier exclusion is removed" also points at a document this file does not contain, so the sentence cannot be read on its own terms.
- **Fix direction (advice):** scope the sentence to the routes it covers, and state in the same breath which routes still revert `ManagerAlreadyUnlocked` inside a batch swap. Replace "The earlier exclusion is removed" with a self-contained statement so the PRD sentence does not depend on a prior version of this file.

### F-07 — The stated reason for dropping the `Compounded` amounts is wrong, so the sentence cannot be copied

- **Location:** B-11 decision line: "Emit `Compounded()` with no fields when fees are compounded into the reserve. Amounts come from the PoolManager events." Body: "The vault does not emit its own swap or liquidity-add event. The PoolManager already emits `Swap` and `ModifyLiquidity` with the vault as `sender`. Filter on that sender for the amounts, price, and liquidity."
- **Why the sentence cannot be copied:** in this repository the PoolManager's `ModifyLiquidity` event carries `(PoolId indexed id, address indexed sender, int24 tickLower, int24 tickUpper, int256 liquidityDelta, bytes32 salt)` — no token amounts (`lib/crane/contracts/protocols/dexes/uniswap/v4/interfaces/IPoolManager.sol:79`). Only `Swap` carries `amount0`, `amount1`, `sqrtPriceX96`, `liquidity` and `tick`. The two-token compound path is precisely the path that does not swap ("Both tokens … do not swap. Deposit the matched pair"), so on that path no PoolManager event reports the compounded token amounts at all. The ABI decision (no fields) is unaffected and remains the locked answer; the justification is what fails. A PRD sentence "Amounts come from the PoolManager events" would be false, and a reader who needs the compounded amount — for an indexer, an accounting view, or a test assertion — would look for a source that does not exist.
- **Fix direction (advice):** keep the locked ABI and replace the rationale with the one that is true — the vault reports no amount; a swap is visible in the PoolManager's `Swap` event, a liquidity change only in `ModifyLiquidity`'s liquidity delta, and the compounded token amounts are not recoverable from the PoolManager events. If an amount is wanted later, that is a separate event decision, not a consequence of this lock.

### F-08 — `Compounded()` from `donate()` is not pinned, and no donation event is named

- **Location:** B-11: "`Compounded()` is emitted when fees are compounded into the reserve. It is not emitted for a maintenance call that compounds no fees." Against "Donation and compound booking": "`donate()` also compounds."
- **Why two readers diverge:** `donate()` collects and redeploys fees, so it satisfies "fees are compounded into the reserve" on one reading and is a "maintenance call that compounds no fees" on another. One implementor emits `Compounded()` from `donate()` when fees are redeployed; another reserves it for `compound()`. The file also never names a donation event, so a PRD writer must decide whether `donate()` emits anything at all, and with what fields — a decision the owner has not been asked.
- **Fix direction (advice):** state whether `donate()` emits `Compounded()` when it redeploys fees, and either name the donation event or state that `donate()` emits none. This one line also closes the emit-side of F-01: once deposits and withdrawals do not compound, `compound()` and `donate()` are the only possible emitters.

### F-09 — "The deployed reserve" names two different quantities

- **Location:** B-10: "The deployed reserve is accounted as one value, `liquidity`, by the Weir method. It is raw V4 liquidity." Against B-06: "`reserves()` and `reserveOfToken` wrap the deployed-reserve reads. There is no local term and no last-committed snapshot. … The deployed read remains the live position: the position's current token amounts plus unpaid fees."
- **Why two readers diverge:** both sentences are individually precise, but they use the same term for a raw liquidity number and for a pair of token amounts. A PRD sentence that says "the deployed reserve" without qualification can be read as the liquidity number, which would make `reserves()` return a liquidity integer instead of token amounts and break `reserveOfToken(address)` for the second token.
- **Fix direction (advice):** give the two quantities distinct names in the PRD — for example "deployed liquidity" for the raw V4 liquidity used by the share formulas and "deployed token reserves" for the `reserves()` view — and use one term for each.
