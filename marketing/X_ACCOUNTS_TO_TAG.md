## Anti-patterns

1. **Hook pollution** — never burn the first 280 chars on a wall of @s.  
2. **Spray and pray** — 8+ tags trains the algorithm (and humans) to ignore you.  
3. **Fake accounts** — always open the profile; check follower quality + org badge.  
4. **Competitor dunk tagging** — invite peers into a shared frame, don’t ratio them.  
5. **Vlad every post** — burns goodwill; save for one flagship.  
6. **Claiming “partner”** — only if you have a real partnership; RH ecosystem list ≠ endorsement of you.
