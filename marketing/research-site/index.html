Product

DETF — Decentralized ETF

ETF-shaped intent: one share over a basket. Onchain reality: a real reserve you can inspect, rules fixed at launch, and no black-box rebalancer rewriting your book.

One share, real basket

Hold one token that stands for a configured onchain reserve — strategy vaults and related assets you can name at deploy. Basket exposure without a discretionary portfolio manager.

Bond to open the market

New units start closed. The first bond opens them and builds shared reserve depth. Bond holders can earn free DETF rewards while locked; free unlocked holders do not get automatic expansion.

Clear rules or open rules

Policy (default): mint only when the unit is rich vs its reserve; burn only when cheap (commonly about ±5%). Open: mint and burn without those price limits. Fees may still apply. Neither mode promises a peg or return.

Rules that don’t move under you

After launch, nobody “owns” the instance to rewrite policy or pause for favor. Bad config means abandon the unit and deploy a new one — not silent edits.

Create your own (premier path)

IndexedEx’s main offer is many DETF designs you can stand up yourself — single strategy, multi-strategy weighted, stable multi, mixed buffer, and more as they ship.

Protocol DETF (fees path)

Same design class, framed as a way to earn a share of protocol fees. Not a separate product category and not guaranteed yield.

What a DETF is

  • One onchain share over a multi-asset (or single-leg) reserve
  • Bond path that opens the unit and deepens shared reserve
  • Deploy-time Policy (price limits) or Open (no price limits)
  • Pricing from the reserve itself, not a private dashboard
  • Many units, many baskets — a deployable product pattern
  • Bond rewards for lockers; optional protocol fee path

What a DETF is not

  • A registered securities ETF or fund share
  • Legal ownership of stocks or other offchain underlyings
  • Guaranteed yield, peg, or “(3,3)” performance
  • Admin-tunable monetary policy after launch
  • Automatic expansion airdrops to free unlocked holders
  • A promise of tight spreads on thin markets

How it works

Three steps users actually take

  1. 1

    Bond

    Take a closed unit live and put capital into shared reserve depth. Further bonds can lock terms set onchain — not discretionary admin favor.

  2. 2

    Mint or burn

    When the unit is live, exchange into or out of DETF against the reserve under Policy or Open rules. Fees may apply. Quotes should match what executes on supported paths.

  3. 3

    Hold, claim, or trade

    Hold the share, claim bond rewards while locked where wired, or trade on a secondary market when one exists. Nothing here invents an APY.

Evidence before hype

What we verified

Claims should rest on measured behavior in controlled runs — not invented mainnet APYs or performance of a live portfolio.

Lifecycle

Closed until first bond

Units start closed. User mint stays blocked until the first successful bond opens the reserve. Waiting alone does not open the market.

Modes

Policy limits · Open does not expand

Default Policy uses rich/cheap bands (commonly about ±5%). Open has no price restrictions when live and does not run extra supply expansion. Policy expansion, when it runs, goes to bond holders only.

Honesty

Quote matched execution

On the supported capital mint path we measured, the previewed amount equaled the executed amount. That is a trust bar — not a yield claim.

Marks

Nested vaults re-mark with markets

Strategy vault shares can track underlying demand when re-marking is wired. Without it, mid prices can lag as volume scales.

Fees

Lag is not free profit

Mark lag is a fairness issue, not an arb coupon. After pool fees and path costs, modest lag often does not pay to close.

Limitation

Opening is not “always mint-rich”

After the first bond, the unit may sit cheap vs its bands. Making it rich enough to mint under Policy can require real inventory activity — not only trading the underlying pool. Documented, not hidden.

Disclaimers

  • Not a registered ETF. “Decentralized ETF” describes a product pattern (basket-like reserve + share). It is not an offer of a securities fund share.
  • No ownership of offchain underlyings. Holding a DETF or reserve asset does not convey legal title to stocks, bonds, or other offchain instruments.
  • No performance promises. Nothing here is investment, legal, or tax advice. Smart contracts and markets involve loss of funds. Mint/burn rules and Policy/Open modes do not guarantee peg stability, liquidity, or returns.
  • Open mode removes price limits only. Primary mint and burn are not restricted by reserve-based price. Fees still apply. Open never runs natural supply expansion. Modes are not a peg or return guarantee.
  • Rewards and reserve growth are mechanisms, not yield products. Extra supply under Policy (when rich) accrues to bond holders. Protocol rewards can deepen shared reserve over time — amounts are not guaranteed.
  • Eligibility. Some underlying tokens may be restricted by issuer or venue rules. Users are responsible for compliance in their jurisdiction.
  • Status. This site explains product direction. Live contract addresses and audits will be linked when published.
