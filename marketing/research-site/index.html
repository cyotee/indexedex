Premier product

DETF — Decentralized ETF

“ETF-shaped” intent: one share over a basket of reserve legs. Onchain mechanics: the diamond is the share ERC-20, the reserve lives in a Balancer V3 weighted pool, and mint/burn rules are priced from that pool — with deploy-time Policy or Open modes.

Bond

First bond takes an instance from inert → live and deepens protocol-owned reserve. Bond terms come from onchain fee/oracle configuration — not discretionary admin favor. Until live, seigniorage mint/burn stay blocked in every mode.

Mint & burn

Pricing engine = the reserve pool, not an off-pool dashboard. Policy (default): mint only when synthetic price is strictly above the mint threshold; burn only below the burn threshold (defaults commonly ±5%). Open: no price restrictions on mint or burn — seigniorage stays available regardless of synthetic price; fees still apply. Zero thresholds never mean Open.

Immutable by design

After deploy, DETF instances are unowned: no instance owner, no diamondCut, no admin pause surface for normal operation. Flawed config → abandon and ship a new package.

What a DETF is

  • Share token over a multi-asset (or single-leg) onchain reserve
  • Bonding path into protocol-owned liquidity
  • Deploy-time Policy (price-gated) or Open (no price gates)
  • Closed-form routes with preview ≈ execution on supported paths
  • Composable with Standard Exchange vaults (opaque protocol legs)
  • Deployable pattern — many DETFs, many baskets

What a DETF is not

  • A registered securities ETF or fund share
  • Legal ownership of offchain underlyings
  • Guaranteed rebase yield or “(3,3)” performance
  • Admin-tunable monetary policy after launch
  • Open still price-checking thresholds (it does not)
  • A promise of tight spreads on thin legs

Evidence before hype

Research spine

Marketing claims should cite measured behavior. Below is the publishable spine from IndexedEx vault / nested-liquidity research — not performance of a live DETF portfolio.

Finding

SE vaults re-mark with markets

Standard Exchange vault share rates track underlying pool demand. Nested Balancer legs that use rate providers keep mids fair; without rates, mids lag as volume scales.

Finding

Rates ≠ free yield

Rate providers are a mark-integrity control. Residual lag is not a free arb until edge clears pool fees and path costs.

Bar

Preview = execution

Closed-form deposit and exchange routes aim for exact preview/execution match (document few-wei only where multi-leg math forces it). Trust numbers the chain can prove.
