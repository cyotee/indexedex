Premier product

DETF — Decentralized ETF

“ETF-shaped” intent: one share over a basket of reserve legs. Onchain mechanics: the diamond is the share ERC-20, the reserve lives in a Balancer V3 weighted pool, and mint/burn rules are priced from that pool — with deploy-time Policy or Open modes.

Bond

First bond takes an instance from inert → live and deepens protocol-owned reserve. Bond terms come from onchain fee/oracle configuration — not discretionary admin favor. Until live, seigniorage mint/burn stay blocked in every mode. Bond positions can earn free DETF rewards; free unlocked holders do not.

Mint & burn

Pricing engine = the reserve pool, not an off-pool dashboard. Policy (default): mint only when synthetic price is strictly above the mint threshold; burn only below the burn threshold (defaults commonly ±5%). Open: no price restrictions on mint or burn; fees still apply; never natural expansion. Zero thresholds never mean Open.

Immutable by design

After deploy, DETF instances are unowned: no instance owner, no diamondCut, no admin pause surface for normal operation. Flawed config → abandon and ship a new package. Protocol bond rewards can compound into more protocol-owned reserve BPT (not a yield coupon).

What a DETF is

  • Share token over a multi-asset (or single-leg) onchain reserve
  • Bonding path into protocol-owned liquidity
  • Deploy-time Policy (price-gated) or Open (no price gates)
  • Closed-form routes with preview ≈ execution on supported paths
  • Policy-only natural expansion to bond holders when synthetically rich
  • Protocol compound of protocol bond rewards into reserve BPT
  • Composable with Standard Exchange vaults (opaque protocol legs)
  • Deployable pattern — many DETFs, many baskets

What a DETF is not

  • A registered securities ETF or fund share
  • Legal ownership of offchain underlyings
  • Guaranteed rebase yield, peg, or “(3,3)” performance
  • Admin-tunable monetary policy after launch
  • Open with price gates or natural expansion (neither)
  • Expansion airdrop to free unlocked DETF holders
  • A promise of tight spreads on thin legs

Evidence before hype

Research spine

Marketing claims should cite measured behavior. Hermetic Single SE DETF scenarios (D0–D9) and prior Uni V2 SE rate-provider matrices — not performance of a live mainnet DETF portfolio and not APY advertising.

DETF · measured

Inert → first bond live

Policy Single SE deploys inert; user mint blocked until first bond creates the reserve. Warp alone does not enable mint or expansion.

DETF · measured

Policy gates · Open never expands

Defaults resolve mint 1.05 / burn 0.95. Open has no price gates when live and never runs natural expansion. Policy expands only when mint-rich, to the bond reward ledger only.

DETF · measured

Preview honesty · protocol compound

Closed-form capital mint: preview equaled execution exactly on the measured path. Protocol compound raises protocol-owned BPT principal — not a claim coupon.

SE · measured

SE vaults re-mark with markets

Standard Exchange vault share rates track underlying pool demand. Nested Balancer legs that use rate providers keep mids fair; without rates, mids lag as volume scales.

SE · measured

Rates ≠ free yield

Rate providers are a mark-integrity control. Residual lag is not a free arb until edge clears pool fees and path costs.

Limitation

Post-bond synthetic drive

In hermetic Single SE, Uni trades alone often cannot clear the mint band after first bond (free-DETF dilution). Production inventory paths matter — documented, not a license to fake prices.

Disclaimers

  • Not a registered ETF. “Decentralized ETF” describes a product pattern (basket-like reserve + share). It is not an offer of a securities fund share.
  • No ownership of offchain underlyings. Holding a DETF or reserve asset does not convey legal title to stocks, bonds, or other offchain instruments.
  • No performance promises. Nothing here is investment, legal, or tax advice. Smart contracts and markets involve loss of funds. Mint/burn thresholds and Policy/Open modes do not guarantee peg stability, liquidity, or returns.
  • Open mode removes price gates only. Primary mint and burn are not restricted by synthetic price. Fees still apply. Open never runs natural expansion. Modes are not a peg or return guarantee.
  • Expansion and compound are not yield products. Natural expansion (Policy only, when mint-rich) accrues to bond holders. Protocol compound deepens protocol BPT and can improve claim backing — amounts are not guaranteed.
  • Eligibility. Some underlying tokens may be restricted by issuer or venue rules. Users are responsible for compliance in their jurisdiction.
  • Status. This site advertises research and product direction. Live contract addresses and audits will be linked when published.
