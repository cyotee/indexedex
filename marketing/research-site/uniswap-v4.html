Why Uniswap V4

Multi-asset products die when capital is trapped in lonely pair pools or when “listing” means a thin portal with no fungible LP. DETFs need:

  • A real book that prices the share
  • Fungible LP that can serve as bond principal
  • Pair-level doors routers already understand
  • Optional vault buffering so cash legs can sit in strategy vaults

Uniswap V4 hooks let that inventory live under familiar pair entry points. We are not trying to rebrand every AMM on earth — just ship the three rails this product needs.

The three rails

1. Pair market · constant-product buffer

Two-asset constant-product energy: one free token (often the DETF share) held in the book, and one pair / cash token that can buffer into a strategy vault. Effective depth is free inventory × vault claim — yield on the vaulted leg can deepen the cash side. Liquidity providers mint one fungible LP. Single-sided deposit and withdraw rebalance through the book when live.

Traders still see a simple pair (share ↔ cash). The vault is inventory under the cash leg — not a second mystery currency on the pool.

For whom: list one share against one pair with real CP depth and LP that can be bond principal — not a wrap/unwrap kiosk with no book.

Launch rail

2. Triangle market · orbital book

Three tokens, one shared inventory, three pair doors into the same book. Pricing uses a spherical / orbital curve so a trade on any pair accounts for the third asset’s state. One fungible LP over all three legs — capital is not split across three lonely pools.

For whom: DETF + two underlyings that should co-exist; multi-asset triangles that should not pay fragmented depth or forced multi-hop tax for no reason.

Launch rail

3. Weighted market · multi-asset book

Two to eight tokens with a weight vector fixed at deploy. Every pair of tokens gets a door into the same weighted book. Joins and exits follow weighted-pool economics — intentional basket math, not pretend-equal stables.

External legs can optionally buffer into strategy vaults. Pair doors still look like normal Uni V4 pairs; the book underneath is shared.

For whom: baskets where equal units would be a lie — sector mixes, intentional crypto weights, multi-leg stories with a clear target mix.

Launch rail

How this meets DETFs

Each launch DETF uses its matching market as the reserve:

  • Pair DETF — share is the free leg; pair token is the cash leg (optionally vault-buffered). Bond principal = fungible CP LP.
  • Triangle DETF — share + two external legs on the orbital book. Bond principal = fungible triangle LP.
  • Weighted basket DETF — share + external legs on the weighted book. Bond principal = fungible weighted LP.

Same product moves everywhere: bond to go live, mint / burn under Policy or Open, hold or claim when wired. Details: How DETFs work.

Premier story stays create your own DETFs. These rails are the market layer under that story — not a portal that only swaps base asset for vault shares with no LP.

Status and honesty

  • Launch set means these three rails are the public product map for first ship — not a promise that every chain is live today.
  • In development as a program: public contract addresses and audits will be linked when published.
  • No APY claims on vault-backed rails. Yield is vault- and market-specific; fees and path costs still apply.
  • Not a registered fund — same honesty bar as the DETF product pages.

Disclaimers

  • Infrastructure, not performance. This page describes market rails and product direction — not investment, legal, or tax advice. Smart contracts and markets involve loss of funds.
  • Not every rail is live. Product map ≠ universal mainnet availability. Live addresses and audits will be linked when published.
  • Rails vs DETF packages. Markets are not the full mint/burn product. DETFs sit on top of these books.
  • No yield or peg promises. Vault yield, sphere curves, and weighted books do not guarantee returns or pegs.
  • Not a registered ETF. Related DETF language describes a product pattern, not a securities fund share.
