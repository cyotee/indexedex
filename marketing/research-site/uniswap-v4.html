Uniswap V4 · market rails · August 2026

Multi-asset markets on Uniswap V4

IndexedEx is building Uniswap V4 market rails: constant-product buffer markets that hold one free token and buffer the other into a strategy vault, pair books on two vaults, and multi-asset books with pair-level doors into one shared inventory. Specs are locked. Public deploys land as each rail is ready. The buffer market is designed so a Decentralized ETF can use it as its on-chain reserve — list the share against a pair token without a separate wrapper portal.

The problem

Multi-asset products need depth that is not trapped in isolated pair pools. Today’s default is expensive:

  • Fragmented capital — three tokens often means three separate books (or six, for four tokens). Liquidity providers rebalance by hand across pairs.
  • Multi-hop fees — traders pay for paths that only exist because inventory was never shared.
  • Listing a share without a real reserve book — a thin portal that only swaps base asset for vault shares has no fungible LP, no constant-product depth, and no natural bond principal for a DETF.
  • DETFs need honest reserves on Uni V4 — a Decentralized ETF prices from a real book. Cash legs should sit in strategy vaults when yield matters; the share leg should list against that book on Uni V4, not only on another venue’s multi-asset pool.

The idea

Use Uniswap V4’s hook model so one shared inventory can serve many pair doors. Swaps still enter through familiar two-token pools; pricing and settlement come from the shared book (or from the bound strategy vault).

What you get

  • Pair-level entry points routers already understand
  • Constant-product buffer books: free token × vault-buffered cash
  • One inventory book for multi-asset rails (not N isolated pools)
  • Fungible LP over the whole book; single-sided deposit and withdraw where designed
  • Deploy-time rules; no admin rewrite surface as the product model

What you do not get

  • A promise that these rails replace every AMM everywhere
  • A wrapper-only portal with no LP book (that shape is not this product map)
  • Concentrated-range NFT positions as the product shape for these books
  • Guaranteed yield, peg, or arb profits from mark lag
  • Mainnet addresses on this page until they are published

The five rails

Five Uniswap V4 integrations form the first public map. Names below are product language — not deploy package labels.

1. Constant-product buffer market

A normal constant-product AMM on Uniswap V4 whose two legs are asymmetric: one free token held directly in the book, and one cash (pair) token buffered into a strategy vault. Effective reserves are free inventory × vault claim on cash — so yield on the vaulted leg can deepen the cash side of the book. Liquidity providers mint one fungible LP. Single-sided deposit (zap-in) and single-sided withdraw (zap-out) rebalance through the book when it is live.

Public swaps still look like a simple pair (for example share token ↔ USDC). The pool does not trade vault shares as a currency; the vault is inventory under the cash leg.

For whom: listing a share (including a DETF) against a pair token with real CP depth, vaulted cash, and LP that can serve as bond principal — not a thin wrap/unwrap portal with no book.

DETF use: a Uni V4 DETF family is designed so the share is the free leg, the pair token is the cash leg, first bond opens the reserve at a deploy-time creation rate, and bond principal is this fungible LP. That DETF surface is a separate product package on top of this market rail.

Spec complete

2. Yield-bearing pair market

A normal pair (for example WETH and USDC) whose effective reserves are claims on two strategy vaults. Deposits buffer into the vaults; swaps buffer in and unwrap out; withdrawals return both pair tokens. Liquidity providers hold one fungible LP over both legs.

Single-sided entry is supported as a rebalance-then-add path: you accept the book’s price impact and vault costs on the way in, and still receive the same dual LP.

For whom: pair liquidity that should sit in yield-bearing vaults, not idle token balances.

Spec complete

3. Three-asset sphere market

Three tokens (often like-kind stables), one shared inventory, and three pair doors into the same book. Pricing uses a spherical curve so every pair trade accounts for the third token’s state. Liquidity providers mint one fungible LP over all three legs.

For whom: multi-stable triangles that should not pay three fragmented books or forced multi-hop paths.

Honest scope: v1 is a single shared orbit — production multi-asset depth on Uni V4, not a full nested multi-orbit concentration system.

Spec complete

4. Four-asset stable market

Exactly four assets on a StableSwap-style curve with amplification fixed at deploy. Up to six pair doors share one inventory. Optional rate sources can put rate-scaled assets (for example yield wrappers) into the same equal-unit book. Zap-in lets depositors add without a perfect four-way ratio.

For whom: four-leg stable or pegged baskets that need native Uni V4 routing instead of living only on another venue’s multi-asset pool.

Spec complete

5. Weighted multi-asset market

Two to eight tokens with a global weight vector set at deploy (each weight at least 1%, weights sum to 100%). Every pair of tokens gets a door into the same weighted book. Joins and exits follow weighted-pool economics (proportional, single-asset, unbalanced) rather than equal-unit stable math.

For whom: baskets where assets should not be treated as equal units — sector mixes, mixed crypto baskets, or other intentional weight stories.

Spec complete

How this meets DETFs

A DETF (Decentralized ETF) is a reserve-backed share with bond, mint, and burn rules. On Uni V4, the first designed family uses the constant-product buffer market as the reserve — not a Balancer BPT book and not a wrapper-only pool.

  • CP buffer reserve — the DETF share is the free leg; the pair token is buffered into a strategy vault. Traders see share ↔ pair on Uni V4. Bond principal is fungible LP from that book. First bond can open the market at a deploy-time creation rate (may start rich vs an abstract peg); later mint/burn follows Policy or Open rules like other DETFs.
  • Yield-bearing dual-vault pairs put two strategy-vault claims on a normal pair so depth can earn on both legs where that topology fits.
  • Sphere, stable, and weighted books give multi-asset inventory with pair-level access for broader baskets as those DETF designs land.

Premier product hierarchy stays the same: the main offer is still create your own DETFs. Protocol DETF remains the path to earn a share of protocol fees (amounts not guaranteed). Uniswap V4 rails are the market layer under that story.

Status and honesty

  • Spec complete means product rules are locked enough to build and review against. It is not a guarantee that a rail is live on every chain today.
  • In development as a program: shipping continues; public contract addresses and audits will be linked when published.
  • No APY claims on vault-backed rails. Yield is protocol- and vault-specific; fees and path costs still apply.
  • Not a registered fund and not legal ownership of offchain underlyings — same honesty bar as the DETF product pages.

Disclaimers

  • Infrastructure, not performance. This page describes market rails and product direction. It is not investment, legal, or tax advice. Smart contracts and markets involve loss of funds.
  • Not every rail is live. Locked specs do not mean universal mainnet availability. Live addresses and audits will be linked when published.
  • Rails vs DETF packages. Market rails are not the full DETF mint/burn product. A DETF family that uses the CP buffer as reserve is a separate package — this page describes the market layer and how that family is designed to use it.
  • No yield or peg promises. Vault yield, stable curves, and weighted books do not guarantee returns, pegs, or arb profits.
  • Not a registered ETF. Related DETF language describes a product pattern, not a securities fund share.
