# Visual lock (unused cinematic drafts)

Not the posting set. X posts use labeled boards in `../infographics/`. These files are scrap from an earlier metaphor pass.

| Role | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Void | `#07080c` | Background |

No HUD, no ticker tape, no candlesticks, no dollar signs, no stock logos, no OHM imagery, no fake app UI, no tiny labels.

## Recurring objects

**DETF coin.** Thick commemorative coin, not a thin dime. Dark bronze edge, warm gold face. The face is a shallow relief of a woven basket holding a few distinct abstract metal tokens (silver, copper, dark steel). No letters unless a later code-built overlay adds `DETF`.

**Reserve vault.** Windowless charcoal stone. A wide shallow basin in the floor. Empty = inert. Liquid metal in the basin = live reserve. Green rim light only.

**Create desk.** One charcoal bench. Three stations left to right: empty die (create), pour into the coin (bond), finished standing coin (use). Same coin as the canonical reference.

## Camera

Museum product photography. Shallow depth of field. Slow moves only: rotation, push-in, lateral pan. One subject per shot.
