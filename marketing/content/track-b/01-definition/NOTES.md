# B01 — What a DETF is

**Audience:** Track B  
**Job:** Stop the scroll. Expand DETF: Decentralized ETF. One token over a basket.

**Hook seed:** ETFs put baskets on TradFi rails. This puts the basket and the mint/burn rules onchain.

**Visual:** Canonical coin, cut open like an architectural model. Inside: a miniature woven basket of distinct metal tokens. Museum light, green rim. No type.

**Motion (6s):** Slow rotation of the cut-open coin. Coin stays sharp. Camera only.

**Caption law:** Body says not a registered securities ETF, and onchain reserve exposure is not legal ownership of offchain stocks. No Olympus in this post.
