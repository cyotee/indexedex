Where the price comes from

A list is not a market

A DETF does not track a spreadsheet. Mint and burn are priced from the reserve pool behind the token.

IndexedEx

Not this

A paper index

  • A list of names and weights.
  • The price lives somewhere else.
  • Someone has to rebalance by hand.

This

A live reserve

  • A pool that holds DETF plus the vault shares in the basket.
  • That pool is the pricing engine.
  • The rules stay put after deploy.

Onchain reserve exposure is not legal ownership of offchain stocks.
