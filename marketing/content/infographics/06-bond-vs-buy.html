Three ways to hold DETF

Buy now, bond, or stake

Buy liquid DETF when you want tokens you can move. Bond for a discounted purchase that stays staked while it vests. Stake DETF you already have.

IndexedEx

Live DETF

Buy

Pay an accepted token. Receive DETF you can hold, transfer, or stake. The quote issues new DETF or swaps through the pool.

Discounted, then vest

Bond

Buy discounted DETF that is funded and staked at purchase. Principal becomes claimable steadily. Rewards can be claimed while it vests.

Already holding

Stake

Deposit DETF, receive the same number of sDETF. Unstake one-for-one for held DETF. No bond period.

Buy, bond, and stake pay or redeem in token units. That is not a promise about market value, APY, or a peg.
