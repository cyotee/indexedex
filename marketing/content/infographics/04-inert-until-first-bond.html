Lifecycle

Inert until the first bond

A new DETF exists but stays off. The first successful bond turns it on and puts assets behind the token.

IndexedEx

1

Deploy

The DETF exists. It is inert. No reserve yet. The rules you set cannot be edited later.

2

First bond

Someone buys discounted DETF that is staked while it vests. That bond turns the DETF live.

3

Live

Hold, mint, burn, stake, or bond more. Anyone can post the first bond, not only the creator.

Bad config: abandon the instance and deploy a new one. There is no admin patch.
