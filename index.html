Provenance

I already shipped the culture war. This is the product.

Olympus proved the world wanted protocol-owned depth, bonding, and a monetary unit with rules. It also proved that a single global token is a blunt instrument — and that “vibes-only” policy does not scale to many baskets.

Hot product energy: yes. One protocol, one money, forever: no. DETFs take the good ideas and turn them into many units you can create — each with a real reserve and rules fixed at launch.

Product

DETF — Decentralized ETF

ETF-shaped intent: one transferable share over a basket. Onchain reality: a real reserve you can inspect, mint and burn against that reserve, and rules nobody rewrites after you enter.

One share. Real reserve.

Hold one token that stands for a configured onchain basket. Pricing comes from the reserve itself — not a private dashboard cosplaying as NAV.

Bond opens the door

New units start closed. The first bond takes them live and builds shared depth. Waiting is not a strategy. Bonding is.

Policy or Open

Policy (default): mint when rich vs reserve, burn when cheap (often about ±5%). Open: mint and burn without those price limits. Fees may still apply. Neither mode is a peg or a coupon.

No admin rewrite

After launch, the instance is not a pet project for someone to pause and rebalance for friends. Bad config? Abandon it. Ship a new one.

Create your own

The main offer is standing up your own DETFs — pair, triangle, or weighted basket designs in this launch. Product, not a single sacred ticker.

Protocol fee path

Want a share of protocol fees? Same DETF design class — amounts not guaranteed. Still a product, not a yield screenshot.

What a DETF is

  • One onchain share over a real reserve
  • Bond path that opens the unit and deepens depth
  • Mint / burn priced from the reserve
  • Policy bands — or Open with no price limits
  • A deployable product pattern, many units

What a DETF is not

  • A registered securities ETF or fund share
  • Legal ownership of stocks / offchain underlyings
  • Guaranteed yield, peg, or “(3,3)” performance
  • OlympusDAO, OHM, or a treasury claim
  • A fork that only changed the logo

How it works

Three moves. That’s the game.

  1. 1

    Bond

    Take a closed unit live. Put capital into shared reserve. Further bonds can lock terms onchain — not discretionary favor.

  2. 2

    Mint or burn

    When live, exchange into or out of DETF against the reserve under Policy or Open. Quotes should match what executes on supported paths.

  3. 3

    Hold, claim, or trade

    Hold the share, claim bond rewards while locked where wired, or trade when a secondary market exists. No invented APY on this page.

We’re launching with a tight stack on Uniswap V4: three market rails (hooks) and three matching DETF products. Each DETF uses its market as the real reserve — list the share, bond into fungible LP, mint and burn against the book.

Share ↔ one pair

Classic two-asset energy: your DETF share lists against one pair token. Cash can sit in a strategy vault under the hood. One fungible LP. Simple story, real depth.

Best for: “one share, one cash leg, go.”

Best for: intentional baskets, not pretend-stable math.

Disclaimers

  • Not a registered ETF. “Decentralized ETF” is product language for a basket-like onchain reserve + share. Not an offer of a securities fund share.
  • Not OlympusDAO / not OHM. Built by the original developer of Olympus. DETF is a new product class. No claim on any DAO treasury or token.
  • No ownership of offchain underlyings. Holding a DETF does not convey legal title to stocks, bonds, or other offchain instruments.
  • No performance promises. Not investment, legal, or tax advice. Smart contracts and markets involve loss of funds. Policy/Open modes do not guarantee peg, liquidity, or returns.
  • Open mode removes price limits on primary mint/burn only. Fees still apply. Open never runs natural supply expansion.
  • Status. Launch families are shipping on Uniswap V4 (pair, triangle, weighted). Not every integration is live on every chain. Live addresses and audits will be linked when published.
